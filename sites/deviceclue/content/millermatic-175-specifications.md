## What are the Millermatic 175 specifications?

For the **230 V Millermatic 175** in Miller's April 2003 OM-1324 revision C, the rated output is **130 A at 20 V DC with a 30% duty cycle at 60 Hz**. The output adjustment range is 30–175 A. The source lists 230 V single-phase input and 19.5 A input at the rated load, with a weight of 33 kg including the gun.

These are specifications from a particular manufacturer document, not measurements of your machine. Check the model, serial number, nameplate and applicable revision, especially when comparing a used machine or a listing with different accessories. Millermatic 135 and 175 are different models; the same document covers both.

## Does 175 A mean continuous welding at that current?

No. The 30–175 A figure is an adjustment range, while the 130 A figure is paired with a stated output voltage and duty cycle. In the cited document, the 30% rating at 130 A is specified at 60 Hz. It does not say that the machine can weld continuously at 175 A. Consult the correct duty-cycle information for the actual unit and operating conditions.

## Does 19.5 A tell me which breaker to install?

No. The figure is input current at the document's rated output, not a universal breaker, outlet or wiring recommendation. The circuit must be suitable for the exact equipment, installation conditions and applicable electrical code. Have a qualified electrician assess the nameplate and installation; do not adapt a plug or wiring based on this table.

## Can I use specifications or settings for a different Miller machine?

Do not assume they are interchangeable. Compare model, serial range, voltage, gun and revision against the manufacturer's documentation. Wire type and diameter, shielding gas, joint and material thickness also affect a welding setup. Our [Millermatic 350P settings article](/guides/millermatic-350p-settings) concerns a different machine and is not a settings chart for the 175. Follow an applicable qualified welding procedure for safety-critical work.
