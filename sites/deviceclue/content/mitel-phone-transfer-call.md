# How to transfer a call on a Mitel 5330 or 5330e phone

On a Mitel 5330 or 5330e using **MiVoice Office 250**, start a transfer during the connected call with the Transfer key, then dial the destination extension. You can speak to the recipient before completing the transfer or send the caller through without that conversation. Match the procedure to the telephone system as well as the handset model.

Transfer handles the caller you are already speaking with. To redirect calls that arrive later, use [Mitel call forwarding](/guides/mitel-phone-call-forwarding) instead.

## Identify the platform before using feature keys

The 5330 name appears in documentation for more than one Mitel platform. The [Office 250 quick reference](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/s3_imports/Devices%20and%20Accessories/IP%20Phones/5300%20Series/5300%20IP%20Phones%20Platform%20Specific%20Documents/MiVoice%20250/6.1/en/Mitel%205330%20IP%20Phone%20QRG_6.1.pdf) identifies a Transfer key. The separate [Mitel Communications Director 5330 reference](https://productdocuments.mitel.com/doc_finder/UG/EN/MCD_5330_QRG_R6.0_EN.pdf) identifies Transfer/Conference and directs SIP users to different instructions.

If your controls or prompts do not match, ask the administrator which platform serves the phone. Do not select a procedure just because its photograph resembles your handset.

## Announce the call before transferring

For the Office 250 example:

1. Tell the caller you are transferring them.
2. Press Transfer while the call is connected.
3. Dial the recipient's extension.
4. Wait for an answer and explain who is calling.
5. Hang up to complete the transfer.

This is often called an attended transfer. If the extension does not answer, use the flashing IC or Call key to return to the original caller, as specified in the Office 250 instructions. Do not hang up merely because the recipient is unavailable.

## Send the call without an announcement

For an unannounced transfer on the documented Office 250 phones, press Transfer, enter the extension and hang up without waiting to introduce the caller. Think about the destination before doing this: a correct extension number does not tell you whether someone is available to answer.

For a first attempt, use a colleague and a test call. Confirm that you can both complete the transfer and recover the caller when the destination is unavailable.

## Transfer to an outside number

The [5320e/5330e/5340e Office 250 guide](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/s3_imports/Devices%20and%20Accessories/IP%20Phones/5300%20Series/5300%20IP%20Phones%20Platform%20Specific%20Documents/MiVoice%20250/6.3/en/5320e5330e5340e_UG_6.3.pdf) adds an outside-line selection between Transfer and dialing the external number. Use the outgoing-line method assigned to your system. A dialing prefix copied from another office may not work on yours.

Confirm that external transfers are permitted before sending business callers to a mobile or another outside destination. If an internal transfer succeeds but an external one fails, report that distinction to the administrator.

## If the caller is lost or the transfer fails

Write down which key you pressed, the prompt displayed, the destination and when you hung up. Also note whether the recipient rang and whether the original caller remained available on a flashing key. These observations are more useful than repeating the process on live calls or resetting the handset.
