# Fisher & Paykel Dishwasher Not Draining? What to Check

If your Fisher & Paykel dishwasher is not draining, start by checking **whether the wash cycle has finished, whether a fault code is showing, and where the water remains**. On a DishDrawer, a blocked filter or a bent drain hose is one possibility. It is not the only one, so standing water alone does not prove that the pump has failed.

For DishDrawer models, Fisher & Paykel lists the filter, drain hose and plumbing connection among the things to check. The exact filter and control layout varies. Use the instructions for your model, and do not force a drawer open during a cycle.

If water is leaking onto the floor or near an electrical outlet, stop using the appliance. Turn off the water and power **if you can do so safely**, and arrange service. Do not handle a wet plug or outlet.

## First, work out what happened

| Observation | Useful next step |
|---|---|
| Water is left in a drawer after the program | Check whether the program completed and inspect the user-accessible filter according to your model’s instructions. |
| The drawer stopped and shows a code | Write down the code and full model number before clearing anything. Use the code chart for that model. |
| A drain hose visible under the sink is sharply bent | Check the accessible hose without pulling the appliance out or disconnecting plumbing. |
| The dishwasher has just been installed | Note this for the installer: the drain connection and installation need to match the manufacturer’s instructions. |
| Water is appearing outside the appliance | Stop using it and arrange service; follow the leak and electrical-safety guidance above. |

A single observation is a starting point, not a diagnosis. For a double DishDrawer, note whether the water remains in one drawer or both. That is useful information for support; it does not by itself identify a particular component.

## 1. Check whether the wash cycle finished

Look at the display or indicator lights before touching the controls. A paused, interrupted or faulted cycle is not the same situation as a completed cycle that left water behind. If you need to pause or cancel a program, follow the procedure for your exact control panel and wait for any indicated pause or drain operation to finish.

Controls differ by model. For example, Fisher & Paykel’s quick-start guide for a particular DishDrawer control describes a drain before the appliance turns off after cancellation. **Do not assume that the same buttons, timing or behaviour apply to every Fisher & Paykel dishwasher.** Do not repeatedly restart cycles just to make the water disappear.

## 2. Check and clean the user-accessible filter

Fisher & Paykel identifies a blocked filter as a possible reason a DishDrawer may not drain. Switch off the appliance’s power before cleaning, following the safety instructions for your model. Remove the dishes and any rack necessary to reach the filter as described in your guide.

Take care around standing water: broken glass or other sharp objects may be present. Remove and clean only the parts the manufacturer identifies as user-maintenance items for your model. The manufacturer’s support guidance describes washing a removable filter with warm soapy water, rinsing it and refitting it. Check that the filter is correctly seated before using the appliance again.

Filter arrangements differ. Do not force a plate, reach into a pump opening you cannot see, or remove parts that your model’s guide does not identify for user maintenance. If the filter cannot be removed or refitted as instructed, stop and seek help.

## 3. Inspect the accessible drain hose

If you can see the drain hose under the sink without moving the dishwasher, look for an obvious bend or compression. A kink can restrict drainage. Do not disconnect the hose if doing so would spill water into an unsafe area, and do not pull an installed drawer out to reach hidden plumbing.

If the hose appears damaged, the connection is inaccessible, or the problem began just after installation, ask an installer or service technician to inspect it. Fisher & Paykel’s support pages also identify a blocked drain connection as a possible cause. A new installation may need its connection checked against the installation instructions; it is not safe to assume that every connection has the same design.

## 4. Treat a displayed code as a separate clue

Record the exact code and model. In Fisher & Paykel documentation for **some DD24SA and DD24SC DishDrawer models**, `A3` is described as a drawer that cannot drain, with the hose or connection among the possible causes. The appropriate fault-clearing controls depend on the model; use its own instructions after the underlying issue is addressed.

`F1` should not automatically be treated as the same error as a drain blockage. For example, an older Fisher & Paykel DD24 guide associates `F1` with an activated flood switch and directs the owner to turn off the water and power and contact service. Other models can use different fault charts. If the code is unclear, or the appliance is leaking or continually alarming, record what you see and contact support rather than guessing at a reset.

If your display shows F1, read our [Fisher & Paykel F1 guide](/guides/fisher-paykel-dishdrawer-f1-error) for the documented flood-warning examples and their model limits.

## What if the filter and hose look clear?

If water remains after you have completed the user-level checks for your model, arrange service. Fisher & Paykel lists a pump problem among possible causes, but a pump cannot be condemned from this symptom alone. A technician can check the drain path and components without you having to remove the dishwasher or work on internal electrical parts.

Have the full model number ready and note the following:

- Which drawer or compartment holds water?
- Did the program finish, pause or display a code?
- Was the appliance recently installed or moved?
- Is the drain hose visible and free of obvious kinks?
- Is there water outside the appliance?

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I force my Fisher & Paykel dishwasher to drain?

There is no single button sequence that can be assumed for every model. Use the cancel or drain instructions for your exact control panel and wait for any operation the manufacturer describes to finish. If the appliance is leaking, continuously alarming or cannot drain, stop and contact support.

### Why is water still in one DishDrawer after a wash?

An interrupted program, a blocked filter or a restriction in the drain path are possibilities. Record which drawer is affected and follow the checks for that model. The location of the water is useful information, but it does not prove which part has failed.

### Does a dishwasher that will not drain always need a new pump?

No. Fisher & Paykel also lists filter and hose restrictions as possible causes. If the model-specific user checks do not resolve the problem, a technician can investigate rather than replacing parts on the basis of one symptom.

### Are `A3` and `F1` the same fault?

Do not assume they are. The manufacturer’s documents describe `A3` as a drain problem on some models and `F1` as a flood-switch signal in an older DD24 guide. Model and generation matter, so check the fault chart for the appliance in front of you.
