# How to type a comma, apostrophe or hyphen on a DYMO LetraTag

A comma, apostrophe and hyphen are available in the **Symbols** menu. The [US LT-100H symbol chart](#where-the-marks-are-on-the-lt-100h) places them in three different rows.

## Open the Symbols menu on either model

On a LetraTag **LT-100H** or **LT-100T**, press **Insert**, select **Symbols** and press **OK**. Use the up and down arrows to change rows and the left and right arrows to move across a row. Press **OK** when the mark you want is highlighted. It appears in the label you are typing. [DYMO LetraTag support](https://www.dymo.com.au/support?cfid=letratag-faqs)

## Where the marks are on the LT-100H

The US LT-100H guide starts its chart at row A, the currency symbols. The row letters below are landmarks in the printed chart. Use the arrows on the label maker to reach the matching symbols. [DYMO LT-100H symbol chart, printed page 7](https://download.dymo.com/dymo/user-guides/LetraTag/LT100H/UG/LT100H_UserGuide_en-US.pdf#page=10)

| Character | Where to find it | Example label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight apostrophe `'` | Row C, last position, just after the inverted question mark | `KIM'S KEYS` |
| Comma `,` | Row D, between the double quote and the period | `BOLTS, SMALL` |
| Period `.` | Row D, immediately after the comma | `REV. 2` |
| Hyphen `-` | Row F, immediately after the plus sign | `BOX-3` |

## Put an apostrophe inside a word

For `KIM'S KEYS`, type `KIM`, insert the apostrophe through **Symbols**, then continue with `S KEYS`.

## Use two marks in the same label

For `BOLTS, M4-8`, type `BOLTS`, insert the comma from row D, then enter a space and `M4`. Open **Insert** again to add the hyphen from row F, then type `8`. Check the label on screen before pressing **Print**. The row letters come from the US LT-100H chart. On the LT-100T, select the characters you see on its screen. [DYMO’s instructions for the 100H and 100T](https://www.dymo.com.au/support?cfid=letratag-faqs)
