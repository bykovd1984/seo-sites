# How to use BD-Live on a Sony BDP-S3700 Blu-ray player

BD-Live on the **Sony BDP-S3700** downloads extras offered by certain Blu-ray discs. Look for the **BD-LIVE** logo on the case. For a disc that includes the feature, the player needs an internet connection, permission to connect for BD content, and a USB drive to store the download. You open the extra from the movie's own menu. [Sony BDP-S3700 operating instructions, page 17](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/res/manuals/4579/f2b13b14d596d6574a73a689b5d4b0dc/45796631M.pdf#page=17)

## Where to put the USB drive

Plug a USB flash drive with at least **1 GB free** into the **front USB jack on the BDP-S3700**. The player uses it as local storage for the disc's downloads. A drive plugged into the TV won't provide storage to the player. [Sony player diagram, page 9](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/res/manuals/4579/f2b13b14d596d6574a73a689b5d4b0dc/45796631M.pdf#page=9) · [Sony BD-Live storage guidance](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00007443)

## Let the disc connect to the internet

1. Connect the player to your network. The BDP-S3700 supports both Wi-Fi and a wired LAN connection.
2. From the player's home screen, open **Setup**, then **BD/DVD Viewing Settings**. Set **BD Internet Connection** to **Allow**.
3. Insert the disc, open its menu and select the BD-Live feature it offers. The name and location of the extra depend on the title. [Sony setup and playback instructions, pages 15 and 17](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/res/manuals/4579/f2b13b14d596d6574a73a689b5d4b0dc/45796631M.pdf#page=17)

## The menu is missing or the screen goes black

If the disc menu has no BD-Live option, check the packaging for that particular edition. If the option appears and opens to a black screen, give it a few minutes. Sony says this can happen while some discs start BD-Live. [Sony BD-Live support](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00007443)

## The player reports a connection error

For a connection error, use **Setup → Network Settings → Network Connection Diagnostics** to check the player itself. If that check passes, confirm the disc permission and USB space. If only one title fails, check the publisher's support information for that release. Its online service may have changed or closed, even though the movie on the disc still plays. [Sony operating instructions, pages 7 and 27](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/res/manuals/4579/f2b13b14d596d6574a73a689b5d4b0dc/45796631M.pdf#page=27)
