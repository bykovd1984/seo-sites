## How do you use a Kenmore oven’s self-clean cycle?

Confirm that your **exact oven cavity** has a self-clean function, then use its model-specific controls and preparation instructions. A high-temperature self-clean cycle locks the door and burns off soil; it is different from a steam-clean programme or manually cleaning an oven.

Kenmore sells different wall ovens and ranges, including models with different functions in their upper and lower cavities. The words “Kenmore oven” do not identify a universal button sequence or cycle length. Find the model label and check the instructions for your specific cavity before starting.

## Before starting a high-temperature cycle

1. **Empty the oven.** Remove cookware, foil, loose items and accessories. Check the instructions for each rack: some models have racks intended to remain in place, while others require removal. If unsure, remove them and check the model guidance.
2. Wipe up loose food and heavy spills as directed by the manufacturer. Do not scrub or displace the door gasket.
3. Remove any commercial cleaner unless your model’s instructions explicitly permit its use with the self-clean cycle. Do not mix cleaning chemicals.
4. Ventilate the kitchen, keep children and pets away from the hot appliance, and follow the model’s safety instructions. If you have birds or other sensitive pets, move them away from the area and seek appropriate guidance before using the cycle.
5. Close the door and select the self-clean function and any duration offered by your model. Use the exact instructions for its controls; do not force a manual latch.

The appliance can become very hot and produce odours or smoke, particularly with heavy residue. If there is a fire, follow emergency guidance and contact local emergency services as needed; do not open a hot oven to investigate.

## What happens during and after self-clean?

Many high-temperature self-cleaning models lock the oven door during the cycle. The display may show a remaining time or lock indicator. A Kenmore product example describes a two-to-four-hour Flex Clean setting, but this is a feature of that model, not a universal duration or a promise that the door unlocks as soon as the timer ends.

Allow the oven to cool fully and the lock to release on its own. Never force the door. Once the cavity is cool and unlocked, the manufacturer’s general cleaning guidance suggests wiping away remaining ash with a damp cloth. Replace only the clean, dry accessories appropriate for the oven.

## Self-clean, steam clean or manual clean?

| Function | What to check |
|---|---|
| High-temperature self-clean | Preparation, rack removal, ventilation, locking and cool-down for your exact model. |
| Steam clean | Your model’s water amount and cycle instructions; do not assume it uses the same preparation or temperature. |
| Manual cleaning | Approved products and surfaces in the model’s care instructions; do not start a high-heat cycle with residues present. |

Some product ranges have differently equipped cavities. A self-clean label for one cavity does not establish that the other offers the same feature.

## Why is the door still locked?

It may need more time to cool after the cycle. Wait for the oven to cool and follow its instructions. If it remains locked after cooling, note the model, any error code and whether a lockout feature is enabled, then consult Kenmore support or a qualified appliance technician. Do not pry the door or dismantle the lock.
