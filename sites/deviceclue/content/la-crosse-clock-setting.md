## How do you set a La Crosse Technology clock?

Start by finding the model number, usually on the back or inside the battery compartment. La Crosse sells both radio-controlled atomic clocks and ordinary digital clocks; their buttons and setting sequences vary. For the **WT-8005U-S and WT-8005U-B**, hold SET, adjust values with UP or DOWN, and press SET to confirm each setting. Other models may work differently.

An atomic clock uses the WWVB radio signal to set its time. You still need the correct time zone and daylight saving setting. If reception is poor, the manufacturer says its clocks also have a manual setting option.

## Set the time on a WT-8005U-S or WT-8005U-B

1. Install two fresh AA alkaline batteries with the polarity shown in the battery compartment.
2. After batteries are installed, the clock searches for the atomic signal for about seven minutes. Buttons do not work during this initial search. Wait for it to finish, or press WWVB SEARCH once to stop it.
3. Hold SET to enter the settings. Use UP or DOWN to change each value and press SET to confirm and move on.
4. Set 12- or 24-hour format, the hour and minutes, then year, month and date. Set the daylight saving indicator to suit your location.
5. Press SET to exit, or leave the buttons alone for 30 seconds. Press ZONE to select Pacific, Mountain, Central or Eastern on this model.

The documented default zone is Pacific, so a correctly received signal can still display the wrong local hour if the zone is not changed. These instructions do not establish the buttons or sequence for a different La Crosse clock.

## Why is the clock an hour out?

Check the time zone first, then whether the daylight saving option matches your location. If the minutes are correct but the hour is wrong, a zone or daylight saving mismatch is a useful clue. It is not a diagnosis of a defective clock.

If the display has the wrong date as well, check the manually entered year, month and day. For a radio-controlled clock, allow time for a signal search and follow the manufacturer's model-specific placement and reception guidance. Buildings and location can affect reception; manually setting the clock does not guarantee it will receive WWVB later.

## The buttons do not respond

On the WT-8005 models, first check whether the initial seven-minute signal search is in progress. The manufacturer's setup page describes how to stop that search. For a blank or weak display, check battery orientation and use the type specified for the actual clock. If your controls do not match this example, look up the model before attempting a reset: resetting is not the same as setting the time and may clear preferences.

For another appliance with a flashing time display, see our [oven clock guide](/guides/zanussi-oven-clock); the controls and procedure are different.

If the issue is a restart or signal reception rather than setting the time, see the [atomic clock reset guide](/guides/la-crosse-atomic-clock-reset) and match its example to your controls.
