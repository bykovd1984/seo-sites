# Fisher & Paykel fridge water filter reset: clear the indicator

If the filter light stays on after you change the water filter, the refrigerator may need its **replacement indicator reset**. First replace the filter with the correct cartridge, then use the combination for the exact refrigerator and control panel. Resetting the light does not itself replace the filter or flush air and residue from the water system.

On the documented **RF730Q** panel, Fisher & Paykel says to press the **filter icon and + together for four seconds**. On certain integrated RS models, the documented combination is different. Match your model before pressing anything: do not assume one sequence works across the brand.

## Before resetting the light

1. Find the full model number and identify the refrigerator, not a dishwasher or washing machine.
2. Replace the water filter with the cartridge specified for that appliance. Follow the instructions supplied with the replacement.
3. Locate the filter-change indicator and its model-specific reset controls. A dispenser lock or door alarm is a different feature.
4. Follow your model's flushing instructions. The amount and whether the dispenser, ice maker or both need flushing vary by appliance.

Fisher & Paykel advises against resetting before replacement because doing so makes the indicator's monitoring inaccurate. The light is a reminder, not a measurement that certifies the water is safe.

## Example: RF730Q filter indicator

In the manufacturer's RF730Q guide, after replacing the filter:

1. Find the control marked with the **filter icon** and the **+** control on the matching panel.
2. Press both together and hold for **four seconds**.
3. Check that the filter icon turns off.

This is an RF730Q example, not a universal Fisher & Paykel reset. If the panel looks different or the model number does not match, use your own model's instructions. Avoid pressing combinations at random: related combinations can operate other settings.

## A different example: integrated RS models

The manufacturer's guide for **RS80A, RS90A and RS9120W** documents pressing the **alarm icon and +** together for four seconds to reset the filter icon. That distinction is exactly why identifying the model matters. Other integrated or freestanding refrigerators can use different controls.

## Why is the filter light still on?

| Situation | What to check |
|---|---|
| The filter was changed but the icon remains lit | Confirm the exact model and that the right controls were held together for the specified time. |
| A different light remains on | Distinguish the filter reminder from the dispenser lock or another alarm. |
| The controls do not match either example | Find the instructions for the complete model number; do not substitute a sequence. |
| Water leaks or the cartridge does not seat | Stop dispensing and contact the manufacturer or a qualified service provider. |
| The indicator clears but water flow changes | A reset clears a reminder; it does not diagnose a filter installation or supply issue. |

Some replacement procedures require flushing. Follow the quantities and pauses for your own model rather than applying a number from a different refrigerator. Only use a safe drinking-water supply, as Fisher & Paykel specifies.

## Does a power reset replace the filter reset?

Do not rely on unplugging the appliance to clear a replacement reminder. The documented procedure is a control-panel reset after the filter has been changed. If it still fails after you verify the model and procedure, contact Fisher & Paykel with the model and a description of the indicator.
