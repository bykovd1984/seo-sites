# Electrolux oven symbols: fan, grill and heating functions

On many Electrolux ovens, a **fan** represents a fan-assisted cooking function, while a **zigzag line near the top** suggests a grill. The complete picture matters: a fan, a fan inside a circle, and a fan with a grill line can represent different functions. Electrolux explicitly says that names and symbols vary between models, so use these clues to find the right entry for your own oven rather than treating them as a universal chart.

## Start with the complete symbol

| Visual clue | What it can indicate | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Fan or propeller | Air circulation as part of a cooking function | Look for a surrounding ring or additional heating lines. |
| Zigzag at the top | A grill function | Your oven may offer full, smaller-area or fan-assisted grilling. |
| Straight lines at top and bottom | Conventional heating elements | Confirm that both lines are present and the model calls it conventional cooking. |
| Bulb | Oven light | A light setting is not necessarily a heating function. |
| Clock or bell | Time or timer setting | These controls are separate from selecting how the oven heats. |

The table describes common visual cues, not exact drawings or a complete feature list. A symbol accompanied by a droplet, snowflake, food picture or letter should be identified in the function list for the precise model: it could denote a special programme rather than ordinary heating.

## Fan symbol or fan with a circle?

A fan indicates moving air, but it does not tell you on its own which heating elements are used. Compare the whole icon with the Electrolux function overview and your model's own control panel. The manufacturer describes hot-air functions that circulate heated air; another icon may combine the fan with a grill or a different heating pattern.

If a recipe specifies fan cooking, choose the matching named fan function on your appliance and follow the recipe's fan-oven temperature where provided. A cooling fan running after cooking is a different issue from the cooking function selected on the dial.

## Which symbol is the grill?

A top zigzag is a useful first clue. Some ovens provide more than one grill: Electrolux shows different full and smaller grill options on certain panels. A fan plus a grill symbol may select a different function from the grill alone. Check the function name and instructions for your oven before placing food inside.

Electrolux's illustrated grill guidance for the controls it describes says to close the oven door and monitor food closely. Follow the instructions for your own model and use heat protection when handling hot shelves and cookware.

## The clock, lock and cleaning symbols

A flashing clock is usually a time-setting question, not a request to choose a cooking mode. See our [Electrolux clock guide](/guides/electrolux-oven-clock) for a documented panel example. A padlock may relate to a control lock, while cleaning programmes differ in both symbol and operation; identify the exact feature before starting it.

For another brand, use the separate [AEG oven symbols guide](/guides/aeg-oven-symbols) or [Zanussi oven symbols guide](/guides/zanussi-oven-symbols); a similar-looking mark is not proof that every feature works identically.

## How to identify an unfamiliar icon

1. Record the full model and product number from the appliance's identification label.
2. Note whether the icon is on the function dial, touch display or clock controls.
3. Compare every element of the icon with the function list for that exact appliance.
4. If the symbol or control panel does not match, use Electrolux support rather than guessing or starting a cleaning programme.
