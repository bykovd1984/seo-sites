## What are the dimensions and weight of a JET JPS-10TS?

They depend on the stock number, wings, rails and revision. In JET’s **March 2008 M-708480 revision C** table, JPS-10TS stock numbers **708480** and **708481** are each listed at **60 × 27 × 40 inches** overall. The steel-wing 708480 has a listed net weight of **205 lb**; the cast-iron-wing 708481 has a listed net weight of **270 lb**.

Do not use those figures for every saw marked JPS-10TS. A later manufacturer document lists different overall dimensions for assemblies with accessories and distinguishes rail lengths. Confirm the saw’s stock number, serial range and fitted accessories before planning a move or allocating space.

## 2008 specification table: two stock numbers

| Specification | 708480, steel wings | 708481, cast-iron wings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall length × width × height | 60 × 27 × 40 in | 60 × 27 × 40 in |
| Table without extensions | 20 × 27 in | 20 × 27 in |
| Table with extensions | 44 × 27 in | 44 × 27 in |
| Net weight | 205 lb | 270 lb |
| Gross weight | 215 lb | 280 lb |

These are the manufacturer’s listed specifications for that document, not measurements of a saw we have physically tested. Gross weight and net weight are different fields; neither should automatically be treated as the weight of a particular used saw with extra accessories fitted.

## Why do other JPS-10TS dimensions differ?

The **May 2014 M-708490 revision C**, for serial number 14053173 and higher, describes several later configurations. It lists fully assembled dimensions with accessories of **60 × 40 × 41 inches** with 30-inch rails and **82 × 40 × 41 inches** with 52-inch rails. Its measurement description is not identical to the 2008 table’s short “overall dimensions” label, so the two entries are not necessarily measurements of equivalent configurations.

The 2014 table also distinguishes saw-only and assembled weights. Use the relevant row for the actual wings and rail set; do not combine the 2008 net weight with the 2014 footprint as if they describe one verified configuration.

## What should I measure before moving it?

Check the identification plate, included rails and extensions, and any mobile base or other accessories. Measure the assembled width, depth and height at their furthest projections, and check the route through doors as well as the final workspace. Use an appropriate lifting and moving plan for the actual weight; never rely on a specification for an unidentified revision as a lifting limit.

Similar-looking JET **JPS-10** listings are not sufficient proof of the specification for a **JPS-10TS**. Verify the complete model and stock number against the manufacturer’s document.
