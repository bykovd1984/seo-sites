# How to check caller ID and the call log on a Panasonic home phone

On the US **Panasonic KX-TGD810 or KX-TGD813**, press the **down arrow marked CID** on the idle handset to see who called. Press down again to start with the newest caller, or up to start with the oldest. On the older **KX-TG2431 and KX-TG2432**, either arrow opens the list, and the screen may offer **Rcvd calls**. [Panasonic KX-TGD810 series, page 40](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/KXTGD81_KXTGD83_PNQX9140XA_ENG.pdf#page=40) · [Panasonic KX-TG2431 and KX-TG2432, page 27](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/KXTG2431_KXTG2432_PQQX14383YA_ENG.pdf#page=27)

## Find a missed call and decide whether to call back

1. Open the caller list using the control for your model. On the KX-TGD810 series, it records information for the latest 50 callers. **Redial** opens a separate list of the last five numbers dialled from the handset. If no external callers ever appear here, [check the Caller ID service on your line](#if-the-phone-rings-but-never-shows-a-number).
2. Scroll to the entry you want. If you only want to check who called, press **Off** when you are finished. The handset can show a missed-call notice even if nobody left a voice message.
3. To return a known caller’s call from the list, select the entry with its telephone number and press the **Talk** key, marked with a receiver. If the caller withheld the number, there is no number in that entry to dial. [Panasonic’s KX-TGD810 series caller-list instructions](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/KXTGD81_KXTGD83_PNQX9140XA_ENG.pdf#page=40) · [Panasonic’s redial instructions](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/KXTGD81_KXTGD83_PNQX9140XA_ENG.pdf#page=16)

## What the display messages mean

| What you see | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Private caller** | The caller asked for their details to be withheld. |
| **Out of area** | Caller information was unavailable from the originating service. The words alone do not identify where the caller lives. |
| A familiar name | If the incoming number matches an entry in your handset’s phonebook, the phone can show the name saved there. |
| **Missed call** disappears | On the KX-TGD810 series, returning a handset to its charger or pressing **Off** can clear the standby notice. Open **CID** to check the list. |

## Why two KX-TG2432 handsets can disagree

On a two-handset **KX-TG2432**, each handset has its own caller list. A call answered or reviewed on one handset can still appear as missed on the other. That explains why two handsets can show different missed-call counts for the same household. [Panasonic’s KX-TG2432 caller-list notes](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/KXTG2431_KXTG2432_PQQX14383YA_ENG.pdf#page=27) · [Panasonic’s KX-TGD810 series display descriptions](https://help.na.panasonic.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/KXTGD81_KXTGD83_PNQX9140XA_ENG.pdf#page=40)

## If the phone rings but never shows a number

Caller ID has to be active on the telephone line. Ask someone whose number is normally visible to call, let the handset ring at least twice, and check the display and the caller list. Some providers deliver the details on the second ring or later. If every call still lacks a number, ask the provider to check Caller ID for your line. If only names are missing, ask whether your service supplies names as well as numbers.
