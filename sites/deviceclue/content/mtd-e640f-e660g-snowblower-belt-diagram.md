# MTD snowblower belt diagram for E600E, E610E, E640F and E660G

If you are looking down at the engine pulley, **Figure 26 on page 16** is the starting point. The belt that turns the auger runs forward to the auger pulley. The belt that moves the wheels runs to the friction-wheel disc. An E640F or E660G has **two auger belts plus one drive belt**. An E600E or E610E has **one of each**. [See MTD’s belt diagrams on pages 16–17](https://c.searspartsdirect.com/doc/L0403353#page=16).

## Which belt belongs on this model?

Read the plate on the rear frame below the engine, viewed from the operator’s position. The following numbers are from MTD form **770-8782A**. They identify what was originally fitted. If ordering a replacement, give the supplier the full model and serial number so they can check any later part-number change.

| Models on the cover | Auger belt | Wheel-drive belt | Exploded parts drawing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E600E, E610E | One 754-0430, item 23 | One 754-0343, item 22 | Page 26 |
| E640F, E660G | Two 754-0430, item 22, listed as matched | One 754-0346, item 21 | Page 27 |

On an E660G with only one auger belt still visible, check both grooves against the drawing. On an E610E the single auger belt is the original arrangement. A tractor-mounted attachment or a single-stage snow thrower calls for its own drawing. [Compare the two MTD parts drawings](https://c.searspartsdirect.com/doc/L0403353#page=26).

## How to read the belt route

Figure 25 locates the plastic cover in front of the engine. Figure 26 shows the upper pulleys and idlers. In the twin-belt version the **front and rear auger belts** each run from their engine-pulley groove to the corresponding auger-pulley groove. Figure 27 shows the two auger positions from below and identifies the idler spring and support bracket. For the **wheel-drive belt**, follow Figure 28 on page 17 to the friction-wheel disc.

If the wheels still drive but snow no longer discharges, identify the auger route first. If the auger turns but the machine has lost traction, inspect the wheel-drive path. These observations help you choose the right diagram. Neither symptom alone establishes that the belt is broken.

## What changes when replacing the auger belts?

Shut down, remove the ignition key, wait for every moving part to stop and allow the engine to cool. Disconnect and ground the spark-plug lead as MTD specifies. Before tipping the machine, follow its fuel-handling instructions. Work away from ignition sources and get service help if you cannot support the machine securely.

MTD starts with the upper belt cover and the lower frame cover removed. On the twin-belt models both auger belts come off the engine pulley. At the auger housing, the idler spring and stop bolt provide the clearance shown in Figures 27 and 28. The rear belt passes through the gap at the support bracket first, then the front belt follows. On reassembly, keep each belt in its own groove and restore the spring and guides. Use page 16 for the complete sequence.

## What is different about the traction belt?

The traction belt comes away from the engine pulley and friction-wheel disc after tension is released at its idler. Figure 28 shows the passage between the wheel and disc. At the end of assembly the support bracket rests on the **stop bolt**. Refit both covers before operating the machine. If the bracket will not return to its seat, a belt runs outside a groove, or a control does not disengage, leave the engine off and recheck the assembly.
