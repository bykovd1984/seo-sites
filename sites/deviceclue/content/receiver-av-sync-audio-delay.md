# How to set A/V Sync on a receiver when dialogue is out of time

If you hear a handclap before the hands meet on screen, the sound is early. A receiver’s **audio delay** can hold it back. If you see the hands meet first, the sound is already late. Check how much delay the receiver is adding and reduce it if you can. On some Sony TVs, the TV’s own **A/V sync** setting can delay the picture, so check which device you are adjusting. [Sony STR-DN1080 A/V Sync](https://helpguide.sony.net/ha/strdn108/v1/en/contents/TP0001221248.html) · [Sony TV guidance for picture and sound timing](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00026069)

## Check which input needs a change

Replay a short scene with a visible clap, knock or close-up of someone speaking. Try a second program on the same input. If a single broadcast channel is affected and the others line up, leave the receiver at its original setting. If a disc player is consistently out of time and a TV app plays correctly, select the player’s input before adjusting anything. Both receivers below remember the delay separately for each input. [Sony’s checks for channels and sources](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00026069) · [Denon AVR-X1800H Audio Delay](https://manuals.denon.com/AVRX1800H/NA/EN/DRDZSYdwbwwnyp.php)

## Set A/V Sync on a Sony STR-DN1080

Open **Setup → Audio Settings → A/V Sync**. With a compatible HDMI-connected TV, choose **HDMI Auto** to let the devices compensate. The manual adjustment runs from **0 to 300 ms** in **10 ms** steps. Write down the starting value. If the sound comes early, increase it by one step and replay the same moment. If one value puts the sound a little early and the next puts it a little late, choose the closer match by ear. [Sony STR-DN1080 settings](https://helpguide.sony.net/ha/strdn108/v1/en/contents/TP0001221248.html)

While watching the TV input, use the receiver’s display-panel menu, `<AUDIO>` → **A/V SYNC**. Sony disables this adjustment in Bluetooth Transmitter mode and during native DSD playback with **DSD Native** enabled. [Sony STR-DN1080 availability and display-panel controls](https://helpguide.sony.net/ha/strdn108/v1/en/contents/TP0001221248.html)

## Set Audio Delay on a Denon AVR-X1800H

Select the input, press **OPTION**, choose **Audio Delay** and press **ENTER**. **Auto Lip Sync** is on by default. Its effect depends on the connected TV. To fine-tune the result, move down to **Adjust** and use left or right. The manual range on this model is **0 to 500 ms**. After changing the setting, replay the scene from the same input before moving to another source. [Denon AVR-X1800H Audio Delay controls](https://manuals.denon.com/AVRX1800H/NA/EN/DRDZSYdwbwwnyp.php)

## If the sound still arrives late at zero

Increasing the receiver’s delay will move the sound later still. Check the TV’s instructions for an adjustment that can hold back its picture. Sony documents this option for compatible TVs with external audio. Compare one setting at a time and keep the previous value so you can return to it. If the timing issue follows a single channel or video across inputs, check another program before changing the whole setup. [Sony TV troubleshooting](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00026069)
