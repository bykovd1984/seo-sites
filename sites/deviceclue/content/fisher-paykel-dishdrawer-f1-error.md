# Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer F1 Error: What It Means

On the Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer models covered by the manufacturer’s guidance discussed here, **F1 is a flood-related warning**. An older DD24 user guide describes the flood switch as activated; Fisher & Paykel’s Australian support page also lists excess foaming as a possible cause on the DishDrawer systems it discusses.

F1 does **not** tell you which part has failed. The safe next step depends on your exact model and whether water is visible. If water has reached the floor, keep clear of wet electrical equipment. Turn off the water and power only if you can do so safely, and arrange service. Never touch a wet plug or outlet.

This guide concerns DishDrawer dishwashers. It does not decode F1 on a washing machine or claim that every Fisher & Paykel dishwasher uses the same fault chart.

## What to do first when F1 appears

1. **Stop the wash and look from a safe position.** Note whether there is water on the floor or around the cabinet. Do not reach behind the appliance or into its base to investigate.
2. **Record the exact code and the model number.** If your DishDrawer has two drawers, note which display shows the fault and whether both drawers are affected.
3. **Follow the fault instructions for your model.** An older Fisher & Paykel DD24 guide tells owners with F1 to turn off the water and power supplies and call an authorised service centre. Do this only where the controls are accessible and dry.
4. **Do not keep clearing the code to run more washes.** A reset does not establish why the warning appeared. If you see water outside the appliance, arrange service.

If you cannot safely isolate power or water, stay clear of the wet area and contact an appropriate professional. Do not pull out, tilt or dismantle an installed DishDrawer to follow a generic online repair method.

## What does F1 mean?

In the older [DD24 US/Canada guide](https://www.fisherpaykel.com/download/user-guide/Kitchen/Clean/DishDrawer/599595B-us-ca-dishdrawer-dd24-guide.pdf#page=34), Fisher & Paykel says F1 indicates that the flood switch has been activated. Its [Australian DishDrawer support page](https://support.fisherpaykel.com/au/s/article/f-code-displaying-on-dishdrawer-dishwasher) identifies flood detection or excess foaming as possible causes in the context of the models covered by that support article.

Those descriptions are clues about a protective warning. They do not establish whether a hose, seal, valve, detergent problem or another condition caused the warning on your particular appliance. The Australian support page does not list every compatible model, so check your appliance’s own fault chart before applying its detailed recovery steps.

| What you can observe | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| F1 appears and water is visible outside the dishwasher | Treat it as a leak requiring attention; isolate water and power if safe and arrange service. |
| F1 appears but you cannot see water | The warning still needs model-specific attention. An apparently dry floor does not identify the cause. |
| The code returns after a model-specific recovery | Record the code and arrange service instead of repeatedly restarting the appliance. |
| The drawer will not drain but no F1 appears | Record the actual symptom or displayed code. A drainage problem is not automatically an F1 flood warning. |

## Can I reset an F1 error?

There is no reset sequence that should be assumed for every Fisher & Paykel dishwasher. Some online advice treats the disappearance of F1 as a complete repair, but clearing a display does not prove that the underlying condition has been resolved.

The manufacturer’s instructions differ by document and situation. Its older DD24 fault chart directs the owner to turn off water and power and contact service for F1. Its Australian DishDrawer support page offers a different recovery path when **no water is visible**. Because that support page does not provide a complete model list, use the instructions matched to your model rather than transferring one procedure to an unknown appliance. If you see water on the floor, the support page says to turn off water and power and book a service.

Avoid removing the drawer, exposing the base or tilting the dishwasher to clear a warning. Built-in appliance servicing can involve water and electrical components. If the instructions for your exact model are unclear, contact Fisher & Paykel or a qualified technician.

## What if the DishDrawer keeps beeping?

First record the displayed code, if there is one, and check the fault instructions for the exact model. Some DishDrawer models use lights rather than a text display; their indicator pattern must be interpreted using the correct chart. Beeping by itself does not identify F1 or prove which part needs repair.

Tell support whether the sound is continuous, which drawer is affected and whether you see water outside the appliance. Do not force a drawer open while it is operating.

## Is F1 the same as a dishwasher that will not drain?

No: **a symptom and a code are different pieces of information**. A dishwasher can fail to drain for several reasons. Some Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer models use `A3` for a drawer that cannot drain, while an older DD24 guide identifies `F1` with the flood switch. Never assume the codes are interchangeable or that an A3 chart applies to an unidentified machine.

If your machine is simply retaining water and shows no flood warning, note the actual code and consult its model-specific drainage checks. Our guide to a [Fisher & Paykel dishwasher that is not draining](/guides/fisher-paykel-dishwasher-not-draining) explains the first checks and when to seek service. If it shows F1, follow the F1 guidance for that model.

## Information to give a technician

Before contacting service, record:

- The full model number and the displayed code or documented light pattern.
- Which drawer is affected and whether there is water on the floor.
- When the warning appeared: during a wash, after installation or at another point.
- Whether the fault returned after following the instructions for that model.
- Whether there was visible excess foam, without assuming it was the cause.

These details can help the technician investigate; they do not establish a diagnosis without inspecting the appliance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does F1 always mean a broken pump?

No. The F1 descriptions cited above concern flood detection or excess foaming; they do not identify a specific failed pump. A technician may need to investigate why the protection activated.

### Can excess detergent cause an F1 warning?

Fisher & Paykel’s Australian DishDrawer support page lists excess foaming as a possible cause in its F1 guidance. That does not prove it is the cause on your machine. Check the detergent and fault instructions for your exact model and avoid repeated cycles while the fault is unresolved.

### Is it safe to keep using the other drawer?

Do not assume it is. Follow the fault and safety instructions for your model; where the manufacturer directs you to isolate the appliance and call service, follow that guidance for the whole appliance. Visible leakage requires attention before further use.
