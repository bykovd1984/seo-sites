# How to scan a folded A3 page with a ScanSnap iX1600 carrier sheet

To save an A3 drawing as one image on a **ScanSnap iX1600**, fold it with the printed face outside, put it in the full-size **Carrier Sheet**, then scan both sides and have ScanSnap Home join them. PFU identifies this accessory as **PA03360-0013**. Its black-and-white band marks the edge that enters the feeder first. The smaller **Photo Carrier Sheet** has a different part number, **PA03770-0015**. [PFU’s carrier sheet identification guide](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/scansnap_help/en/pc/topic/ope_basic_carrier_sheet.html) · [PFU’s illustrated iX1600 loading sequence](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/cloud_android/ix1600/en/sscloudmobile/topic/scan_a3cs_larger_a4_ix1600.html)

## Position the drawing inside the carrier

Use a sheet that can take a crease. Fold it accurately in half with the drawing visible on both outside faces, then smooth the fold. Lay the carrier in front of you with the patterned band at the top and the thick black mark at the upper right. Open it and slide the drawing in with its top edge along the top of the carrier and the fold at the right. A skewed fold can leave the two halves of a straight line at different heights in the finished scan. [PFU’s iX1600 placement diagrams](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/cloud_android/ix1600/en/sscloudmobile/topic/scan_a3cs_larger_a4_ix1600.html)

## Set ScanSnap Home to produce one image

In **ScanSnap Home for Windows or macOS**, edit the profile you will select on the scanner. Set document detection to treat all sheets as documents and choose **Duplex**. In the feed options, use automatic document-size detection. Open the **Carrier sheet** settings and set **Save as** to **one double-page spread image**. Choose the required output paper size, confirm the changes and save the profile. These are the desktop profile settings for combining the two faces of a folded document. [PFU’s desktop carrier sheet settings](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/scansnap_help/en/pc/topic/ope_scan_set_larger_a4_cs.html?model=ix1600)

## Feed the patterned edge first

Open the iX1600’s input chute and extend the output stacker. Load the carrier with the **black-and-white edge first**, bring the side guides against it and select the saved profile. Scan once. Open the result and follow one printed line across the middle. It should continue onto the other half in the same image. Two separate pages are a reason to check the profile’s **Save as** selection before folding the original again. [PFU’s iX1600 feeding instructions](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/cloud_android/ix1600/en/sscloudmobile/topic/scan_a3cs_larger_a4_ix1600.html) · [PFU’s desktop output options](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/scansnap_help/en/pc/topic/ope_scan_set_larger_a4_cs.html?model=ix1600)

## Use a different output setting for a photograph

A small photo or clipping can lie flat in a compatible full-size or Photo Carrier Sheet. For a postcard with handwriting on the back, choose the carrier option that saves the front and back separately. That preserves the picture and the message as two images. Check your scanner model before borrowing the iX1600 sequence. For example, the portable **iX100** also supports a carrier, but a folded large sheet needs two passes on its single-sided scanner. [PFU’s photograph and clipping instructions](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/imaging/downloads/manual/scansnap_help/en/pc/topic/ope_scan_set_photo_cs.html) · [PFU’s scanner compatibility and pass counts](https://scansnap-faq.pfu.ricoh.com/hc/en-us/articles/12928438388377-Can-I-use-ScanSnap-Carrier-Sheets)

## If part of the drawing disappears at the fold

Open the carrier and check where the fold sits. If it projects beyond the carrier edge, ScanSnap can omit a narrow strip around the join. PFU suggests placing the fold about **1 mm inside** that edge and rescanning. Compare a feature that crosses the fold, such as a grid line or lettering. A visible seam or slight skew can remain, especially with a thick original.
