# What a 32°F display means on a Dimension One @Home spa

**Stay out of the spa if the temperature display reads 32°F (0°C) on the @Home control covered by Dimension One’s 2005 guide.** The controller has detected a problem, and the actual water temperature is unknown. Before reporting the reading, [check the unit shown on the panel](#check-whether-the-display-uses-fahrenheit-or-celsius). Contact an authorized Dimension One Spas dealer or service center. The guide gives the same instruction for **132°F (55°C)**. [Dimension One @Home Series owner’s guide, printed page 11, hosted by The Spa Works](https://www.thespaworks.com/sites/default/files/-2005-2011_d1_at_home_owners_manual.pdf#page=14)

## Read the SETPOINT light

On this control, the normal display shows water temperature. When the **SETPOINT** light is on, it shows the temperature you selected. A few seconds after an adjustment, it returns to the water reading.

## Check whether the display uses Fahrenheit or Celsius

Note which screen showed the number and whether the spa uses Fahrenheit or Celsius. For example, **32°C** is about **90°F**. The low-end warning in this particular guide is **32°F**, which appears as **0°C** on a Celsius display. [Dimension One’s display and setpoint indicators, printed pages 8–10](https://www.thespaworks.com/sites/default/files/-2005-2011_d1_at_home_owners_manual.pdf#page=11) · [Temperature units and display warnings, printed page 11](https://www.thespaworks.com/sites/default/files/-2005-2011_d1_at_home_owners_manual.pdf#page=14)

## What to tell the service center

Report the spa model and year if known, the exact number and unit, and any flashing lights or letters such as **OH**. A photo of the full panel can capture all of these display details. Keep the spa out of use while the temperature is in doubt.

## Match the control to its model year

The display warning above comes from the 2005 @Home Series guide. Dimension One lists guides by collection and year, so a dealer can match a later @Home control to its own instructions. [Dimension One’s model and year directory](https://www.d1spas.com/manuals)
