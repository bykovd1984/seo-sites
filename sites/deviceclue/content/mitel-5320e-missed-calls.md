# How to see missed calls on a Mitel 5320e desk phone

Start with the 5320e at its idle screen. If your office uses **MiVoice Business**, press **Menu**. On **MiVoice Office 250**, press **Applications**. On either system, select **Call History**, then **Missed**. Use the key immediately to the left of an entry to see the time, date and caller details. ## Read the missed-call entry

1. In **Missed**, use the navigation keys below the display to turn the page. The lower-left number gives the total in this list. The lower-right numbers show which entries are on screen.
2. Open the entry and check its timestamp. A name and number appear when that information is available. On MiVoice Business, an entry marked `2x` can show two call attempts.

## Return a call from the list

To return the call, select **Dial** in the details. If MiVoice Business requires a prefix for an outside line, add your office’s prefix to the displayed number first. Select **Close** to return to the list without placing a call. ## Why an older call can disappear

The Call History application keeps the **50 most recent calls across all types**. Answered and outgoing calls take up space alongside missed calls. Once the list fills, the next call replaces the oldest entry.

## Where to look if the call was answered

If a colleague answered the call at this handset, look under **Answered**. If your 5320e shows different menus, ask which phone system serves your extension so you can match its guide.
