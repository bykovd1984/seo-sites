# Uniden BC75XLT all lockout: channels, scanning and keypad

When a Uniden BC75XLT shows **ALL Locked Out**, it cannot currently scan an available programmed channel. Uniden's support FAQ explains that the message can occur when there are **no channels programmed**. It is also useful to check whether programmed channels or their banks have been excluded from scanning. The message is different from a locked keypad.

Before changing settings, note the exact display and keep a record of any programmed frequencies. Do not start by clearing the scanner's memory: an empty scanner still needs valid frequencies to scan.

## Which kind of lock are you seeing?

| What you see | First distinction |
|---|---|
| `ALL Locked Out` when starting scan | Check that channels contain programmed frequencies and the relevant banks and channels are available. |
| `T/L/O` on a channel | The BC75XLT documentation identifies temporary channel lockout. |
| `L/O` on a channel | The channel may be permanently locked out from scanning until unlocked. |
| Keys do not respond normally | Check the keypad-lock indicator and the model's keypad-lock instructions; it is not the same as channel lockout. |
| A channel is available but silent | Scan availability is different from reception, frequency, range and radio-system compatibility. |

## Check whether channels are programmed

Press **Hold** and inspect the selected channel. Uniden's BC75XLT example shows an empty channel with a zero frequency. If no suitable channels have been programmed, add frequencies appropriate to the services you are legally allowed and technically able to receive. A list for another region, or a trunked/digital system outside the scanner's capabilities, is not proof it will work locally.

Uniden's model-specific programming example is: press **Hold**, select a channel, press the orange **Function** key and release it, then press **E** to enter programming mode. Enter the frequency including its decimal point and press **E**. Once appropriate channels are entered, press **Scan**. Consult Uniden's support instructions if the display or controls do not match.

## Unlock a particular channel

For a BC75XLT channel showing a temporary or permanent lockout, Uniden describes selecting that channel, pressing **Hold**, and pressing **L/O** until the lockout indication no longer appears. Press **Hold** again to return to scanning or searching. Check the selected channel carefully so that you do not change a channel you deliberately excluded.

Temporary and permanent locks behave differently: on the **BC75XLT**,  cycling power clears temporary channel lockouts according to Uniden, but does not make an empty channel programmed or necessarily clear permanent exclusions. The model also offers a broader unlock action affecting registered channels in enabled banks; use it only if you intend to restore all of them.

## Still no scanning or no audio?

Confirm the relevant bank is enabled, at least one channel has an appropriate frequency, and the channel is not excluded. If the scanner now scans but you hear nothing, investigate reception separately: an out-of-range frequency, incompatible system, weak signal or antenna issue will not necessarily show as a lockout message. Record the message, model and affected channel before contacting Uniden support.
