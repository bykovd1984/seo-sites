# What do the symbols on an OMRON blood pressure monitor mean?

On the US OMRON **BP7250**, the plain heart flashing as the cuff measures is the pulse indicator. A heart with curved lines and a small **2** or **3** beside a finished reading is the irregular-heartbeat indicator. A flashing **OK** on this model refers to a phone connection or data transfer. On the European **M3 Comfort HEM-7155-E**, a cuff drawing marked **OK** tells you how the cuff was fastened. Check the model code and when the mark appeared before using the display key. ## A heart with a small 2 or 3 on the BP7250

The small number counts detections within that one measurement. **2** means the monitor detected an irregular rhythm twice. **3** means three or more times. Your pulse rate is the separate number beside **PULSE /min**. Arm movement can also bring up the irregular-heartbeat symbol. If it keeps appearing, show the dated readings to your clinician, who can assess the rhythm. [OMRON BP7250 instructions, page 16](https://omronhealthcare.com/storage/pdfs/5-series-wireless-upper-arm-blood-pressure-monitor-bp7250-im-en_3608778-6j.pdf#page=16)

## A moving figure or a loose cuff

The seated figure with wavy lines on the BP7250 means it detected body movement. If it appears with a result, remove the cuff, wait two to three minutes, and refit it. Keep your arm still for the next reading. On the M3 Comfort, an open cuff drawing signals a different problem. Reapply the cuff more snugly and take a fresh measurement. A closed cuff with **OK** indicates sufficient tightness. ## Why the high-reading indicator differs between models

| Model and market | What switches on the indicator |
| --- | --- |
| BP7250, US | **HIGH** appears when SYS reaches 130 mmHg or DIA reaches 80 mmHg. |
| M3 Comfort HEM-7155-E, Europe | The high-reading symbol appears when SYS reaches 135 mmHg or DIA reaches 85 mmHg. |

For example, **126/82** triggers the BP7250 indicator because the lower number crosses 80. That same pair is below both M3 display thresholds. These values explain the displays. If the numbers keep differing from office readings, see [how OMRON handles accuracy checks](/guides/omron-calibration). Use your clinician's advice when deciding what to do about your own readings. [OMRON BP7250 indicator threshold](https://omronhealthcare.com/storage/pdfs/5-series-wireless-upper-arm-blood-pressure-monitor-bp7250-im-en_3608778-6j.pdf#page=16) · [OMRON M3 Comfort indicator threshold](https://www.omron-healthcare.co.uk/storage/im-hem-7155-eo-en-05-01-2022.pdf#page=20)

## AVG when you open the BP7250 memory

If **AVG** flashes while you look back through BP7250 readings, the monitor is showing an average. It uses the latest two or three readings taken within the most recent ten-minute window. To copy one reading into a log, select its individual memory entry and check the date and time. If your monitor carries a different model number, OMRON lists its guides by the full code, including several different 5 Series units.
