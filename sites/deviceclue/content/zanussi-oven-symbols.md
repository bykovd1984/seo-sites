## What do Zanussi oven symbols mean?

Zanussi ovens can offer conventional heat, fan cooking, grill, defrost and cleaning functions. **The set of symbols varies by model**, so match the entire icon to the function list for your oven's model and product number code (PNC). A fan alone does not establish whether a heating element is on.

Zanussi's UK support page explains the functions below for its single and compact oven examples. It also shows the associated icons; not every feature is fitted to every oven or cooker.

## Fan, conventional heat and grill

| Function | What it does in Zanussi's explanation |
|---|---|
| Conventional cooking | Uses top and bottom heating elements. |
| True Fan Cooking / FanCook | Fan cooking intended for everyday meals, including cooking on multiple levels. |
| Bottom heat | Uses the lower element; useful for finishing the base of some foods. |
| Grill | Uses the grill for browning or cooking smaller portions. |
| Turbo or fan grilling | Combines the fan and grill for circulating heat. |
| Defrost | Uses a fan to circulate room-temperature air without heat in the described function. |

Use the [Zanussi function guide](https://support.zanussi.co.uk/support-articles/article/zanussi-oven-and-cooker-functionssymbols) to compare the actual images, then verify against the specific model. A fan-with-grill symbol is not interchangeable with a plain grill setting, and a defrost symbol should not be guessed from a fan shape alone.

## Which symbol is the grill?

Zanussi shows a full grill and a separate fan-grill function in its guide. Compare the whole pictogram on your selector with the manufacturer images and check the model's cooking instructions, including whether the door should be open or closed. Do not rely on a generic description if the markings have worn away.

## What is the self-clean symbol?

Cleaning functions differ. Zanussi's general page describes Aqua Clean, which uses water and heat to loosen residue on supported ovens; other products may offer a different cleaning system. A cooking icon is not proof that a particular cleaning programme exists. Identify the model and follow its preparation, rack and ventilation instructions before starting a cleaning cycle. Never add water or run a high-temperature cycle solely because an icon looks similar.

## Is a clock symbol a cooking mode?

Not necessarily. Clock and timer controls can set the time of day or a timed cooking period rather than select an element. If the display flashes after a power cut, see our [Zanussi oven clock guide](/guides/zanussi-oven-clock) for the documented +/− panel example. The control sequence can differ on other panels.

If your appliance is an AEG rather than a Zanussi, our [AEG oven symbols guide](/guides/aeg-oven-symbols) covers separate model limitations; do not assume its icons match your oven.
