## Why is my HP DesignJet T520 not printing?

First separate a job that **does not print at all** from one that prints only part of a drawing or has missing colours. Check the T520 front panel for its exact message, the paper and ink status, and the computer's print queue. A stuck or paused job, a disconnected printer and a document-size mismatch lead to different checks.

This guide covers HP DesignJet T520 printing symptoms, not every HP printer with a similar name. The computer menus and driver options vary by operating system. Record any front-panel error before restarting or changing settings.

## Start with the symptom

| Symptom | First place to look |
| --- | --- |
| No job comes out and the queue says offline or paused | Printer status, connection and selected queue. |
| A job stays in the queue | Open the queue and inspect the job status; a failed job may block later ones. |
| An error appears on the front panel | Match the exact message to HP's T120/T520 guidance before clearing it. |
| Only part of the page prints | Compare the document page size, selected paper size, margins and driver preview. |
| The sheet feeds but colour or lines are missing | Investigate print quality separately from a connection failure. |

If the panel reports a paper, ink or carriage problem, resolve that message using the exact model's HP guidance. Do not reach into a moving carriage or force jammed material. Persistent mechanical faults need service.

## The printer is offline or the job is stuck

Confirm that the T520 is powered on and select the intended printer queue, especially if the computer has several copies of the same printer. For a networked printer, check the printer's reported network status and the connection used by the computer. HP's T120/T520 wireless guidance distinguishes a ready connection from an offline one.

On Windows, open the print queue and check whether printing has been paused or the queue is set to work offline. If an individual job is stuck, try cancelling that job through the queue and then send a small test document. On a shared printer, coordinate with other users before clearing jobs or restarting the print service: those actions can affect their work. Follow HP's operating-system-specific steps if the queue remains stuck.

## The T520 prints only part of the page

Open the source document and compare its page dimensions and orientation with the selected roll or sheet and the printer driver's paper size. Inspect the print preview and scaling or fit settings before wasting another large sheet. Printable margins and application-specific clipping can matter. Try a small document with a known size to distinguish a file or application issue from a broader printer problem.

A partially printed drawing is not the same diagnosis as a printer that never receives a job. If the issue occurs in only one application, note the file type and driver settings for support.

## Blank output, missing black or poor print quality

Check the exact ink or printhead message and use HP's T120/T520 print-quality diagnostic guidance. A blank page or missing colour can involve several causes; do not assume an empty cartridge or repeatedly run cleaning cycles without checking the displayed status and HP's instructions. If the diagnostic report itself is defective or an error persists, provide the report and error text to a technician.
