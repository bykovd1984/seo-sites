# Miele G 643, G 663 and G 843 dishwasher dimensions and cutout sizes

The US **fully integrated** installation drawings for these Miele dishwashers show a cabinet opening **23 5/8 inches wide**. The appliance itself measures **23 9/16 inches** across. The width is shared by both model groups, but a G 843 uses a different height drawing from a G 643 or G 663.

## Compare the opening with the appliance

| Measurement in the drawing | G 643–G 663, page 9 | G 843–G 863, page 10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cabinet opening width | 23 5/8 in | 23 5/8 in |
| Cabinet opening depth | 23 5/8 in | 23 5/8 in |
| Cabinet opening height shown | 32 5/16 to 34 1/4 in | 33 1/16 to 35 1/16 in |
| Appliance width | 23 9/16 in | 23 9/16 in |
| Appliance body depth shown | 22 7/16 in | 22 7/16 in |

A space measuring exactly 23 9/16 inches across matches the body width; it is **1/16 inch narrower** than the specified opening. Measure the clear width at more than one point to catch a cabinet side or trim piece that narrows the opening. [Miele’s dimensioned installation drawings, printed pages 9–10](https://us.mieleusa.com/MieleMedia/docs/operatinginstructions/dishwashers/retired%20models%20manuals/installation%20instructions/Full_Integrated_install.pdf#page=9)

## Why the height range alone can give the wrong answer

On the **G 643–G 663 drawing**, Miele says the black leg extensions can be removed for a **machine height of 32 1/4 to 33 inches**. The white feet must remain fitted. The supplied legs cannot produce machine heights from **33 1/4 to 34 inches**. For example, if the installation requires the machine itself to stand at 33 1/2 inches, check the extended-leg requirement before treating the cabinet height as a fit.

On the **G 843–G 863 drawing**, Miele calls for extended legs for a machine height in the **35–37-inch** range. These notes refer to the height of the machine on its feet. Measure the cabinet separately from the finished floor to the underside of the counter.

## Allow for the furniture door

The **22 7/16-inch** arrow measures the dishwasher body. A furniture panel and handle sit at the front of a fully integrated installation. For clearance in front of the unit, measure to the planned face of the fitted door and check that the door can swing past the adjacent trim and toe kick.

## Does the counter need a steam deflector?

Before fitting the machine, also check the exposed underside of the counter. The stainless-steel **steam deflector** protects that edge when the dishwasher door opens. Miele says a solid waterproof counter, such as granite, does not require it for steam protection. If the counter has a joint between materials, the strip should cover the joint. On these models the strip also carries a reflector for the operating light, so check its alignment with the control-panel indicator. The drawing and material notes are on page 11 of the installation instructions.

## Match the full model before measuring

Check the rating plate for the complete designation. For example, Miele identifies the **G 643 SCVi** and **G 843 Vi** in its [US Incognito operating instructions](https://us.mieleusa.com/MieleMedia/docs/operatinginstructions/Dishwashers/Retired%20models%20manuals/Operating%20Instructions/Dishwasher%20G643_843_us.pdf). Use the installation sheet that matches the machine and cabinet front. A nominal 24-inch description is too coarse to settle a difference of a sixteenth of an inch.
