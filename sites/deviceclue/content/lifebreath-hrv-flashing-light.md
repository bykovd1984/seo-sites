# Lifebreath HRV flashing light: red indicator and control checks

A flashing light on a Lifebreath HRV control is **not one universal fault code**. First identify which light is flashing, the wall control and ventilation-unit models, and whether the unit is operating. Lifebreath describes a flashing **RH** indication during a particular dehumidistat function. Separately, it says a flashing main control or timer on a **non-functional** unit usually points to a wiring, voltage or communication issue that needs a service contractor.

The two descriptions are not interchangeable. A flashing red light by itself does not identify the fault or prove that the system is in defrost.

## Identify what is flashing

| Observation | Useful next step |
|---|---|
| `RH` flashes on a digital control | Check the dehumidistat setting and whether the unit is running; Lifebreath describes this as a high-speed dehumidistat indication on its FAQ. |
| Main control or timer flashes and the unit is not working | Record the control and unit models and contact a service contractor; the manufacturer lists wiring, voltage or communication as possible causes. |
| A red mode light flashes while the unit runs | Match the exact light and control to the unit's instructions; a light can have different meanings on different controls. |
| Display is blank | Check the documented normal display and power status for your model. A blank display is not the same symptom as a flashing RH indicator. |
| The light returns after a restart | Record the sequence and ask for service rather than repeatedly restarting the unit. |

## What does a flashing RH mean?

In Lifebreath's FAQ, flashing **RH** on a digital control indicates that the appliance is operating at high speed in its dehumidistat function. Once indoor humidity reaches the RH setting, it returns to its previous speed and operating mode. This describes the named indication and function; it does not mean every flashing red light on every HRV is normal.

An HRV or ERV is not a standalone dehumidifier. Lifebreath notes that persistent indoor humidity can require other equipment or further investigation. If the display reading seems wrong or the home remains humid, record the setting and conditions for a qualified contractor rather than assuming the indicator alone identifies the cause.

## Could a flashing red light mean defrost?

Some Lifebreath equipment and controls describe indicator behaviour during defrost, but the applicable light, colour and pattern depend on the exact model and control. A defrost explanation found for a 500-series document should not be transferred to a different wall control simply because both have red lights. Match the model and its indicator table first.

If the equipment is not operating as expected, or the light pattern is not documented for that combination, ask a contractor to diagnose it. Do not open the electrical enclosure, disconnect wiring or bridge terminals to test an indicator.

## What to tell the technician

1. The HRV or ERV model and the wall-control or timer model.
2. The exact label, colour and pattern that flashes; a photo can help.
3. Whether air is moving and whether the display responds normally.
4. The RH setting, if relevant, and when the behaviour began.
5. Any other message or recent power interruption.

This information separates the displayed symptom from an assumed cause. A contractor can use it to identify the correct control and unit documentation before diagnosing the fault.
