## “Pro Series” is not the full model

Before buying cells or pulling on a cover, identify the thermostat’s full model number. Similar-looking T6 products can have different battery arrangements and power requirements.

The comparison below comes from two Resideo documents. It gives a concrete reason to check the model rather than repeat an online instruction that says every Honeywell thermostat takes the same batteries.

| Documented family | Battery arrangement in its guide | Source to match |
|---|---|---|
| T6 Pro Programmable | Two AA alkaline cells | 33-00182EFS |
| T6 Pro Z-Wave | Three AA alkaline cells | 33-00588EFS |

This is not a complete catalogue of Honeywell Home thermostats. Do not transfer the arrangement to a different model simply because the faceplate looks familiar.

## Replace batteries on a matching T6 Pro

Read the battery section for the exact variant before removing the thermostat. The documented families use a removable thermostat body on a wall mount; the removal instruction must be followed without forcing the mount or pulling attached wiring.

- Prepare a complete fresh set of the specified alkaline AA cells.
- Follow the guide to release the thermostat body from its mount.
- Match each cell to the polarity marks in the compartment.
- Refit the body as instructed and check the display.

Do not detach the wall base or move wires for a routine battery change. If the body does not release as shown, stop and confirm the model. A sliding battery compartment on another family is not evidence that this one opens the same way.

## Will changing the batteries erase the settings?

The linked guides distinguish the time and day from other stored settings. They state that replacing cells within two minutes avoids resetting the time and day; the other settings described in those guides are stored without battery power.

That is model-specific information, not a reason to hurry through an unfamiliar removal procedure. Have the cells ready, work carefully, and verify the displayed time and operating mode after refitting.

## If the display is still blank

Check the cell type, polarity and seating against the compartment markings. Confirm that the thermostat body is correctly refitted. A fresh battery set does not establish that the wall power, mounting connection or thermostat electronics are sound.

Some installations use external power, with batteries serving a different role from a battery-only installation. Use the correct power section of your model guide rather than assuming that a blank display has one cause. Do not move thermostat wires as a speculative fix.

## Avoid an unnecessary reset

A factory reset and a battery change are different tasks. Resetting can change user or installation settings, so it should not be the automatic response to a battery warning. If normal operation does not return, record the full model, warning text, power arrangement if known, and what happened after replacement for support.

If the display says recovery rather than low battery, see [what Honeywell recovery mode means](/guides/honeywell-thermostat-recovery-mode).
