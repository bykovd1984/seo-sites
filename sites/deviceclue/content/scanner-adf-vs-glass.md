# Scanner ADF vs. glass for loose pages, books and photos

Use the **automatic document feeder (ADF)** for a stack of loose, flat pages. Use the **scanner glass**, also called the flatbed, for a book, a fragile photograph or a small clipping. The feeder pulls each sheet past the scanner. On the glass, the original stays still while you scan one side. On the **Brother MFC-L2750DW**, the feeder accepts longer sheets, and the glass supports a higher optical resolution. ## Use the feeder for a stack of letters

For a stack of office correspondence, remove fasteners, fan the pages and stagger their leading edges. On the MFC-L2750DW, load them **face up, top edge first**, then slide the guides to the width of the stack. Brother rates the feeder for up to **50 pages of standard 20 lb paper**. Put torn, taped or badly wrinkled pages aside for the glass. A long US Legal sheet can go through this feeder if it meets the other paper requirements. The glass takes originals only up to 11.8 inches long, so a 14-inch Legal sheet extends beyond it. [Brother’s feeder instructions and paper limits](https://support.brother.com/g/s/id/htmldoc/mfc/cv_mfcl2750dw/use/html/GUID-23283B84-E92E-4782-B32E-5E7328CFEBF6_27.html) · [Brother’s glass dimensions](https://support.brother.com/g/s/id/htmldoc/mfc/cv_mfcl2750dw/use/html/GUID-A8CD1E0D-FF8A-460E-8802-048A8192B422_28.html)

## Use the glass for a photo or a book page

Empty the feeder first. On this Brother model, put the side you want to scan **face down** and align the page with the **upper-left corner** of the glass. Lower the cover gently over a book. For a small receipt, the flatbed also avoids the feeder’s minimum width of 4.1 inches. If the scan shows a blank back or the wrong sheet, check the face direction and look for a page still sitting in the feeder. Other machines can use a different alignment corner or feed direction. ## Choose the source for two-sided originals

The MFC-L2750DW reads both sides in a single pass through the ADF. Select two-sided scanning in the scan settings and send one sheet with content on both sides first. Check that the second side is present and upright before loading the rest. On the glass, turn the sheet yourself for the second scan. When comparing another machine, look specifically for **two-sided scanning** in its specifications. Two-sided printing describes what happens to the output paper. ## When fine detail matters

Brother lists an optical scan maximum of **1200 × 1200 dpi** from the glass and **600 × 600 dpi** from this feeder. If the goal is to enlarge a small handwritten annotation or a photo, the glass gives you the higher optical setting. For ordinary letters, first look at a scan at the size you expect to read it. If the smallest print is clear, the feeder can save you from placing each remaining page by hand.
