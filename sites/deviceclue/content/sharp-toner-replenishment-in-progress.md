# Sharp “Toner replenishment in progress” on the MX-2314N, MX-2614N and MX-3114N

When this message appears during setup, **leave the front cover closed, keep the copier powered on and wait for the message to clear**. Sharp's installation manual explains that the machine is checking the supply of toner from the cartridges to the developing unit. For this step it gives a range of **30 seconds to 6 minutes**. A cartridge replacement has its own prompt. [Sharp installation manual, section 2-5, hosted by Manuals+](https://manuals.plus/m/6b9c430396c57a9012df8dfc30218f2dcd5f9865e247b46cbbac93a82f74f772)

## What those six minutes apply to

The installation sequence places the message immediately after the cartridges have been fitted and the front cover has been closed. The time varies with the amount of toner and how it is distributed inside the cartridge. The signal to continue is the **message disappearing**. Sharp's range applies to that installation step. [Sharp installation manual, main-unit toner supply check](https://manuals.plus/m/6b9c430396c57a9012df8dfc30218f2dcd5f9865e247b46cbbac93a82f74f772)

## Should you open the front cover or restart?

Leave the copier running with the cover closed. Opening the cover, entering service mode or switching the power off and back on can interrupt the supply check. Sharp warns that doing so can produce toner errors or feed too much toner. [Sharp installation precautions, section 2-5](https://manuals.plus/m/6b9c430396c57a9012df8dfc30218f2dcd5f9865e247b46cbbac93a82f74f772)

## Does the display ask for a new cartridge?

- **“Toner replenishment in progress.”** Wait for the supply check. Leave the cartridge in place while it runs.
- **The remaining-toner estimate reads 25–0%.** Have a spare in the indicated color ready. You can see the estimate by holding the **COPY** key while printing or while the copier is idle.
- **“Change the toner cartridge.”** Replace the color identified on the display. **Y** is yellow, **M** magenta, **C** cyan and **Bk** black. After a replacement, the copier automatically runs an image adjustment.
- **“Replace used toner container.”** This refers to the waste toner box, a different part from a fresh toner cartridge. [Sharp MX-2314N operation guide, toner and waste toner sections](https://secure.sharp.eu/cps/rde/xbcr/documents/documents/Marketing/Operational_manuals/MX-2314N_OG_UK.pdf) · [Sharp MX-2614N and MX-3114N operation guide, pages 1-61 to 1-64](https://sharpone.sharp.co.uk/api/pim/download/03c5a45a-5ba9-4844-ba99-4a992d8c3bef)

## If the copier never returns to ready

Note the model, how long it has been waiting, what happened immediately beforehand and any new error code. Give those details to your copier service provider. In particular, say whether the message followed installation, a cartridge change or an ordinary print job. That tells the technician which operation was in progress.
