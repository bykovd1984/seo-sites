# American Standard Washbrook urinal dimensions and rough-in connections

The **Washbrook FloWise Universal** is nominally **18⅞ inches wide** and projects **14⅛ inches** from the finished wall. The wall connection depends on which bowl you have. The **6590.001US** takes its flush connection at the top. The **6515.001US** takes it through the back. They share an outline, but the water supply enters at different places.

## Read the overall dimensions

| Dimension on the Washbrook sheet | US units | Metric as printed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Width across the front | 18⅞ in | 480 mm |
| Projection from the finished wall | 14⅛ in | 360 mm |
| Overall vertical reference on the front view | 26⅛ in | 664 mm |

The 26⅛-inch reference spans the fixture in the drawing. To mark the outlet on a wall, start from the finished floor and use the outlet centerline in the side view. To check how far the fixture extends into the room, start from the finished wall. The rough framing and the finished tile face can give different measurements.

## Choose the matching inlet drawing

| Model on the specification | Inlet | Drawing to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6590.001US | ¾-inch top spud | Top-spud view showing the connection above the bowl |
| 6515.001US | ¾-inch back spud | Back-spud view showing the supply connection at the rear |

A *spud* here is the flush-water inlet. The waste outlet is separate. Its detail shows a **2-inch inside-threaded outlet connection** and gasket. Use the dimensioned detail for the hanger slots and outlet center. The [original US-suffix rough-in drawing](https://manualsdump.com/en/manuals/american_standard-washbrook_flowise_universal_urinal-usa13/54900/2) shows the two views on the same sheet.

## What the flush-volume range describes

American Standard lists **0.125–1.0 US gallons per flush** for the fixture. The bowl is supplied separately from the flush valve. To work out the consumption of an installed unit, identify the fitted valve and its rated flush volume. A ¾-inch inlet size alone cannot tell you how much water the valve delivers.

## Which dimensions to use for a replacement

Record the fixture number, inlet location, available width, projection and the position of the existing outlet. Compare those measurements against the matching manufacturer drawing before choosing a replacement. The nominal fixture envelope alone cannot establish whether the existing valve and hangers align. The [American Standard dimensioned specification](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/documents/specsheets/167744_as_us_bath_spec__6515__6590_6627__original.pdf#page=2) shows the finished-wall and finished-floor references beside each view.
