## How do you change a Craftsman garage door remote battery?

First identify the **handheld remote**, not just the motor unit on the ceiling. For the documented Craftsman Security+ **139.53752 / 139.53753** remote instructions, the replacement parts list names a 3 V 2032 lithium battery. Open the housing at its indicated end, insert the battery according to the polarity marks inside, and close the case securely. Other remotes and replacement transmitters may use a different battery or opening method.

Keep coin cells away from children. If a battery may have been swallowed or inserted into the body, seek emergency medical help immediately. Dispose of used cells safely according to local rules.

## Replace the battery in the documented Security+ remote

1. Find the remote’s own model number and check that it matches the 139.53752 / 139.53753 instructions. The opener’s model number alone is not enough to identify the remote.
2. At the end indicated in the manufacturer diagram, carefully use the visor clip or a small screwdriver to separate the case. Do not force the housing or damage the circuit board.
3. Remove the old cell and fit the specified replacement, matching the positive and negative markings inside the cover or on the circuit board. Do not infer polarity from a different remote.
4. Reassemble the case so it closes securely. Test the remote while the garage door is fully visible and the opening is clear of people and objects.

This example concerns the original remote described in the cited Craftsman instructions. A third-party compatible remote can look similar while taking a different battery. Read its own markings and instructions before buying or opening it.

## Remote battery, keypad battery or opener backup?

The remote is the small handheld transmitter. A wall-mounted wireless keypad is a separate device, while a backup battery in the opener powers the motor system during an outage on equipped models. These batteries are not interchangeable, and the remote procedure above is not a backup-battery replacement procedure.

## Does a new battery require reprogramming?

Do not erase the opener’s memory merely because you changed a remote battery. Test the remote first. If it still does not work, verify the battery type and orientation, try another working control if available and check the model-specific instructions. Our [Craftsman remote programming guide](/guides/craftsman-garage-door-remote-programming) covers a documented pairing example; holding LEARN can erase stored controls on some openers.

A weak battery is one possibility, not a diagnosis. If the wall control also fails, or the door moves unexpectedly, use the [garage door troubleshooting guide](/guides/craftsman-garage-door-troubleshooting) and arrange qualified service when needed.
