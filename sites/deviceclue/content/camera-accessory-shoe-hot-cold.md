# What can you connect to a camera accessory shoe?

An **accessory shoe** is the bracket that holds a flash, microphone or another accessory. Whether the camera can trigger the flash or record the microphone through that bracket depends on the type of shoe and the accessory's connections. [Canon's accessory shoe glossary](https://www.canon.com.au/get-inspired/glossary/accessory-shoe)

## How to tell a hot shoe from a cold shoe

A **cold shoe** provides the mounting rails. A **hot shoe** adds electrical contacts so the camera can communicate with compatible equipment, for example by triggering a flash when the shutter fires. On a camera cage or bracket, check whether the shoe has a connection back to the camera. A cold shoe can hold an off-camera flash that gets its trigger through a separate cable. [Canon on hot and cold shoes](https://www.canon.com.au/get-inspired/glossary/accessory-shoe)

## Will a flash fit a Canon EOS R6 Mark II?

The **EOS R6 Mark II** has Canon's Multi-Function Shoe. Look up the exact flash model before sliding it into the rails. For example, Canon specifies the **AD-E1** adapter for the **Speedlite 580EX II** and **600EX II-RT** on this body. If you own one of those flashes, the adapter goes between the camera and the flash. Use the camera's accessory list to check a different model. [Canon EOS R6 Mark II shoe and adapter instructions](https://cam.start.canon/en/C012/manual/html/UG-01_Preparations_0090.html)

## Does a shoe-mounted microphone need a cable?

Two microphones that fit the same camera illustrate why the full model name matters.

| Microphone on the EOS R6 Mark II | How audio reaches the camera |
| --- | --- |
| Canon **DM-E100** | Mount the mic and plug its 3.5 mm connector into the camera's external microphone input. The shoe supports the microphone. |
| Canon **DM-E1D** | The Multi-Function Shoe carries digital audio and supplies power to the mic. The camera's microphone input stays free. |

Canon lists both microphones for the R6 Mark II. [Canon microphone compatibility chart](https://cam.start.canon/en/H002/accessory_0020.html) · [DM-E100 connection](https://asia.canon/en/pro-imaging/stereo-microphone-dm-e100/main/product) · [DM-E1D connection](https://asia.canon/en/consumer/multi-function-shoe-directional-stereo-microphone-dm-e1d/main/specification)

## If the mounted microphone seems silent

With a **DM-E100**, push the connector fully into the microphone input. With a **DM-E1D**, check that the mounting foot has clicked into the Multi-Function Shoe and that its locking lever is secure. On the camera, check that movie **Sound recording** is enabled, then record and play back a short clip before the shoot. [Canon R6 Mark II sound recording](https://cam.start.canon/en/C012/manual/html/UG-04_Shooting-2_0080.html) · [Canon shoe attachment instructions](https://cam.start.canon/en/C012/manual/html/UG-01_Preparations_0090.html)
