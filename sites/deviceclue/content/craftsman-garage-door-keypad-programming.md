## How do you program a Craftsman garage door keypad?

Identify both the **garage door opener model** and the **keypad model**, confirm compatibility, and follow the instructions for that combination. Adding a keypad PIN, changing a PIN and erasing the opener’s entire memory are different tasks.

On the documented Craftsman **139.54915**, a compatible keyless entry can be programmed through the specified motion-detecting wall control. The example below applies to that opener and control, not every Craftsman keypad. Keep the door visible and its path clear while programming and testing.

## Example: use the wall control on model 139.54915

The manufacturer’s guide describes the following method for adding, reprogramming or changing a keyless-entry PIN using the documented motion-detecting control panel:

1. Confirm the opener and wall-control type against the model guide and have a compatible keypad ready.
2. Press **LEARN** on the wall control to enter programming mode.
3. Press **LEARN** on that wall control again. Its command LED flashes once.
4. Enter a four-digit PIN of your choice on the keypad, then press **ENTER**.
5. The opener lights flash, or two clicks are heard, to confirm programming according to the guide. Test the PIN with the door area clear.

This is the **wall-control** method in that guide. Do not transfer it to a different wall control, or assume the keypad needs no preparation. Some third-party keypads require their own programming steps and compatibility checks before pairing.

## How is a PIN change different from a full reset?

| Goal | Action to look for in the matching instructions |
|---|---|
| Add a compatible keypad | Program a keyless entry or PIN. |
| Change a known PIN | Use the keypad’s documented PIN-change method, if supported. |
| Remove access after a lost control | Check the opener’s memory-erase procedure and which devices it removes. |
| Add a handheld remote | Follow the remote-programming procedure, not the keypad’s PIN sequence. |

For model 139.54915, holding the **opener-mounted** LEARN button until its indicator goes out erases all stored remote and keyless-entry codes. That is not required merely to try the wall-control method above. The implications and the model-specific erase sequence are covered in our [Craftsman reset guide](/guides/craftsman-garage-door-opener-reset).

## Why will my keypad not program?

Check the exact keypad and opener models, battery condition and whether the keypad lights respond. Re-read both sets of instructions: a universal keypad may require its own preliminary setup, and a keypad intended for another generation might not be compatible. Work through the documented sequence again without holding the opener’s LEARN button.

If the wall control and a paired remote also fail, the problem may extend beyond the keypad. Our [garage door troubleshooting guide](/guides/craftsman-garage-door-troubleshooting) helps separate control problems from safety and mechanical symptoms. If the door binds, has damaged cables or moves unpredictably, stop testing and seek professional service. Do not adjust springs or bypass safety sensors.

## Can I program a remote and a keypad separately?

Yes, where the opener and accessories support it. Each has its own controls and instructions. See the [Craftsman remote programming example](/guides/craftsman-garage-door-remote-programming) for the same documented opener. Verify every control after programming, without publishing or sharing the PIN.
