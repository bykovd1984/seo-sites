# Hitachi washing machine C1 and C2 errors: supply or drainage

On the Hitachi **SF-240XWV, SF-200XWV and SF-180XWV** top-load washers, C1 indicates a water-supply problem and C2 indicates a drainage problem. Start with the relevant external hose and supply or drain arrangement. The code identifies the operation that needs attention; it does not establish which component has failed.

Check the model and exact characters before acting. These are documented top-load examples, not a universal Hitachi code table. Do not apply their drain arrangement to a front-load machine or interpret C02 as C2 without checking that model.

## C1 means water is not being supplied

[Hitachi's washing-machine support FAQ](https://www.hitachi-homeappliances.com/sg/support/products_faqs/washing-machines.html) associates C1 with absent or very slow incoming water. Check whether the tap is open, the supply hose is restricted and the inlet filter needs attention.

Start with checks that do not require dismantling the machine: confirm that household water is available and inspect the accessible hose for a kink. If you need to disconnect a hose or clean an inlet filter, stop the machine, isolate power and close the water tap first. Follow the maintenance steps for your model.

Note whether the machine receives no water or a weak flow. This is useful information for service if the simple checks do not resolve the warning.

## C2 means water is not draining

The [SF-240XWV/200XWV/180XWV documentation](https://www.hitachi-homeappliances.com/my/SF240XWV_200XWV_180XWV_EN.pdf) directs attention to the drain hose and outlet. Look for an accessible kink, obstruction or installation that does not match the specified route.

Do not move a water-filled machine or detach its drain hose without planning for the water inside. If clearing the visible route does not help, leave pump and internal-hose work to service.

Drain height is model-specific. Some Hitachi top-load systems use a low drain arrangement, while other washer designs require a raised outlet. Copying a height from another machine can create a new drainage problem.

## Other codes need different checks

For the same SF-XWV models, the manufacturer's table also distinguishes these conditions:

| Code | Documented area to check |
| --- | --- |
| C0 | Excess laundry load |
| C3 | Open lid |
| C4 | Load balance and stable installation |
| C8 | Lid cannot lock |
| C9 | Lid cannot unlock |

Do not apply the C1 water-supply checks to every code. A locked lid must not be forced, and a safety switch must not be bypassed to continue a cycle.

## Restart after addressing the cause

Once an accessible cause has been corrected, follow your model's normal restart procedure and observe the next attempt. A warning disappearing briefly is not enough: check whether the machine completes the stage that previously failed.

Avoid repeatedly resetting a washer that immediately displays the same warning. Record whether the failure occurs during filling, washing, rinsing or draining, and whether water remains inside.

## When to request service

Contact Hitachi support if the external checks do not restore operation, the lid remains locked, or you see an unfamiliar code. Provide the full model number, a photo of the display and the cycle stage. Stop using the machine if there is leakage, a damaged cable or abnormal electrical behavior; do not open the cabinet to investigate.
