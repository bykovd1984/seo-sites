# AEG Oven Symbols: Grill, Fan and Other Settings Explained

An AEG oven's symbols tell you which function you are selecting, but **the available functions and the way they are shown depend on the model**. A triangular or zigzag mark at the top can indicate a grill; a fan inside a circle is a common symbol for fan cooking. Check the full symbol and your oven's own function chart before choosing a setting.

This guide explains common cooking, grill and timer functions. It also covers what to do when a symbol has worn off or when you have an older AEG Competence oven.

## Quick guide to common AEG oven functions

These descriptions are starting points, not a promise that every AEG control has the same markings. AEG's [illustrated function guide](https://support.aeg.co.uk/support-articles/article/symbols-and-functions-on-your-aeg-oven-or-cooker?locale=en-GB) shows examples; compare them with the chart for your exact oven.

| Function | What it does | Useful distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Grill / full grill | Applies heat from above for grilling, toasting or browning. | A top row of triangular or zigzag marks is a common grill symbol. AEG's Dutch guide shows triangles for grill on its example panel. |
| Turbo grill / fan grill | Combines a fan and top grilling heat. | A fan with a grill-like mark is not necessarily the same setting as ordinary fan cooking. Check the exact icon. |
| True Fan cooking | Circulates hot air for general cooking. | A fan within a circle is a common True Fan symbol, but a fan picture can also appear in other functions. |
| Conventional / top and bottom heat | Uses upper and lower heat for traditional cooking. | Look for your model's top-and-bottom heating symbol rather than assuming a fan is involved. |
| Bottom heat | Heats from below, useful for browning the base of a dish. | It is distinct from a combined fan-and-bottom-heat setting. |
| Defrost | Uses a defrost function where provided. | A fan or snowflake-like mark can be confused with another setting; verify the chart and food-safety instructions. |
| Steam or SteamBake | Adds or uses steam on ovens equipped for that function. | Steam cooking, reheating and steam cleaning are different functions. |

Function names and graphics can differ across generations, including AEG Competence ovens. If your panel doesn't match these examples, use its model-specific instructions rather than guessing.

## Which symbol is the grill on an AEG oven?

Look for a mark representing the heating element at the **top** of the oven symbol. Depending on the panel, it can look like a line of small triangles or a zigzag. AEG's [oven-function guide](https://www.aeg.nl/taste/discover/steam-oven/ovenfuncties-aeg/) illustrates triangles for a grill setting; compare the whole marking with your own chart before turning the dial.

If a fan appears alongside the grill mark, you may be looking at **turbo grill**, which combines fan circulation with grilling heat. On ovens covered by AEG's [grill guidance](https://support.aeg.co.uk/support-articles/article/using-the-oven-grill-function), ordinary grill and turbo grill are separate options. Some models also offer fast grilling. The recipe, shelf position and correct setting depend on the oven and food.

AEG's linked guidance says to grill with the oven door closed on the electric ovens it discusses. Follow the instructions for your specific appliance, especially if you have an older cooker or a different type of oven; don't assume all designs use the same door position.

## What does the fan symbol mean?

On many ovens, a fan surrounded by a circle indicates a fan-heating function. AEG calls a common version **True Fan cooking**: a fan circulates heated air around the cavity. The manufacturer's guide describes it as an everyday cooking function and says the available settings vary by model.

Don't identify a function from the fan alone. A fan beside a grill mark can indicate fan-assisted grilling, while another fan symbol may belong to a different function. Match the surrounding lines and marks, and check whether the control is a heating-function dial or a separate button.

## What setting should I use for baking?

For a recipe that specifies conventional oven heat, look for **conventional cooking / top and bottom heat** on your model. If it specifies a fan oven, look for the model's fan-cooking setting. AEG describes both on its [function guide](https://support.aeg.co.uk/support-articles/article/symbols-and-functions-on-your-aeg-oven-or-cooker?locale=en-GB).

Some recipes give separate conventional and fan temperatures. Use the recipe's stated temperature, shelf position and cooking time for the relevant function; check your model's guidance when adapting a recipe. A fan icon combined with a grill or another mark should not automatically be treated as standard fan baking.

For a different appliance, our [Beko oven symbols guide](/guides/beko-oven-symbols) explains that brand’s examples separately. Use the chart for the oven in front of you.

## What about the timer and clock symbols?

Timer controls answer a different question from cooking-function symbols. A **minute minder** can be a countdown alert; a **cooking duration** can control how long a supported oven runs. An [AEG oven guide](https://www.aeg.co.uk/services/eml/asset/eef60fbf-9b1c-4914-ba60-efc4cbe9044a/E4RM3Q/260127KHI2/PDF/260127KHI2.pdf#page=8) distinguishes these functions for the oven it documents; AEG's [timer support page](https://support.aeg.co.uk/support-articles/article/how-to-set-an-oven-timer-aeg) shows examples of different control types.

The exact symbols, whether a function switches the oven off, and the steps to set or cancel it depend on the panel. Check your model's clock-function chart, especially if the time is flashing after a power cut. Do not assume a countdown has switched off the heating function.

## What does the cleaning symbol mean?

Some AEG ovens have a dedicated cleaning programme. On ovens equipped with pyrolytic cleaning, [AEG says `PYR` can be a reminder](https://support.aeg.co.uk/support-articles/article/oven-display-shows-pyr) to perform that cleaning function. Other models use different cleaning methods or have no self-cleaning programme.

Do not start a cleaning cycle just because a symbol resembles another oven's icon. Check that your model supports the function, then follow its complete preparation, accessory-removal and ventilation instructions. Pyrolytic cleaning uses very high heat and locks the door on the ovens described by AEG.

## How to identify a faded or unfamiliar symbol

1. **Find the full model number and product number.** Look for the data label on the appliance; its location can vary. Record the complete number rather than only the word “Competence”.
2. **Identify the control.** Is the mark next to a heating-function dial, a temperature control or the clock display? On a double oven, note whether it belongs to the upper or main oven.
3. **Compare the complete symbol** with AEG's illustrated guide and the function chart for your exact model. A dot, fan, line or extra triangle can distinguish two settings.
4. **If the marking is worn away**, consult AEG using the full product number. Don't test unknown functions with food inside or infer a setting solely from the dial's position: layouts differ.

AEG's [support search for product instructions](https://www.aeg.co.uk/support/user-manuals/) can help you find the model-specific chart. If the displayed mark is an error code rather than a cooking function, note the exact letters or numbers before looking up the fault.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are AEG Competence oven symbols all the same?

No. Competence identifies a family of ovens, not one control panel. Use the complete model and product numbers to find the correct function chart, particularly for older or double ovens.

### Is the grill symbol the same as the fan oven symbol?

No. A grill function uses top grilling heat; ordinary fan cooking circulates heated air. A third option, turbo or fan grill, combines grilling heat and a fan. Compare all parts of the symbol for your model.

### Does a steam-looking symbol mean self-cleaning?

Not necessarily. Steam cooking and steam-assisted cleaning are different functions, and not every oven has either. Check the label and function description for your model before adding water or starting any programme.

### Why are there different symbols for the top and bottom ovens?

On some double ovens, each cavity has its own control and available functions. Identify which oven the dial operates and consult the relevant section of the model's function chart; don't assume the two cavities offer identical settings.
