## How do you set a Fisher & Paykel oven clock?

The button sequence depends on the oven. On the **OB60SC6CEX2**, set a flashing clock with the − and + controls, then touch the cooking-time and stop-time controls together for two seconds to confirm. On the **OB90S9LEX1**, use the left and right scroll controls and the select button instead. The steps are not interchangeable.

After a power cut, a flashing display often means the time needs to be set again. Some Fisher & Paykel ovens will not operate until their clock is set; that alone does not establish an oven fault.

## OB60SC6CEX2: set the flashing time

1. After power is restored, the model’s display flashes `0:00`.
2. Touch − or + to set the time of day.
3. Touch the cooking-time and stop-time controls **together for two seconds**. The display stops flashing and the oven beeps to confirm.

To change an already-set time on this model, touch the same two time controls together for two seconds until the centre dot flashes, change the time with − and +, then touch the two controls together again for two seconds to confirm. Identify the two time controls using the model’s own panel diagram: the minute minder is not necessarily the same control.

## OB90S9LEX1: scroll and select controls

After power is restored, the manufacturer describes a flashing 24-hour option. Use the right scroll button to choose 24-hour or 12-hour display and press select. Set the time with the left and right scroll buttons, then press select again. A steady time indicates that it was set.

To change an existing time, hold both scroll buttons together for three seconds to open the preferences menu. Follow the display through the clock-format selection, then set the time with the scroll buttons and confirm with select. If the buttons or display differ, use the correct procedure for your model.

## The clock still flashes or the oven will not heat

Check that you confirmed the time rather than only changing the digits. Also check for an ongoing power interruption. On a model that requires the clock to be set, finish setting it before investigating a heating problem. If the clock loses its setting repeatedly or controls do not respond with power available, arrange service rather than removing panels or working on live wiring.
