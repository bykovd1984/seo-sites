## Where is the reset button on a Hoshizaki ice machine?

On some Hoshizaki KM control boards, an **ALARM RESET** button is on the internal control board; it is not necessarily an external button or present in the same place on another model. The manufacturer describes board-level reset and troubleshooting for technicians. Do not open electrical covers or reach inside a running machine to look for it.

A reset can clear a protective lockout without correcting its cause. Before requesting service, record the full model and serial number, any repeating beeps and the conditions when ice production stopped. If the alarm returns, repeated resets are not a repair.

## Alarm reset, power switch and test button are different

| Control or action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Alarm reset | A control-board function on certain machines; its use and conditions depend on the exact board and alarm. |
| User-accessible power switch | Turns the machine on or off as directed for that model; it is not necessarily an alarm reset. |
| Output test | A technician diagnostic function on some control boards, not an alarm-reset button. |
| Bin control | Stops production when the bin is full on applicable installations; it is not a reset to defeat. |

Hoshizaki's KM service documentation illustrates separate ALARM RESET and OUTPUT TEST buttons on particular boards. Other models and revisions can differ. The manufacturer's technician guidance also describes conditions under which a three-beep safety alarm requires a manual reset, but that does not make the same procedure appropriate for every machine or alarm.

## What can an operator check first?

Check the user-accessible power switch, whether the bin is full and whether a visible water supply valve is open, if safe. Do not bypass the bin control, disconnect hoses or remove panels. If there is water near electrical components, avoid the wet area and contact service; isolate supplies only if you can do so safely.

For a machine that has stopped producing ice without an obvious alarm, use the [no-ice checklist](/guides/hoshizaki-ice-machine-not-making-ice). If it beeps, count the repeating pattern before anything clears it and compare the model-specific examples in our [beeping guide](/guides/hoshizaki-ice-machine-beeping).

## Why does the alarm come back after a reset?

The underlying condition may still be present, the machine may have a different fault, or the reset may not apply to that control. A pattern alone does not prove which part has failed. Hoshizaki describes a three-beep long-freeze alarm for particular KM control systems; its diagnosis involves checks meant for trained technicians. Arrange service rather than repeatedly clearing the alarm to keep making ice.

## What should I tell the technician?

Give the complete model and serial number, beep count and pause pattern, whether ice is present in the bin, when the issue began, and recent cleaning or service history. Note whether the machine has already been restarted. This information helps a technician identify the correct control and preserve the clues needed for diagnosis.
