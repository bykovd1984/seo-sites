# How to set the date and time on a VTech DS6751 phone

You can change the clock from either the handset or the telephone base on a **DS6751, DS6751-2 or DS6751-3**. Use the idle screen. If you are listening to a message or talking on a call, finish first so that MENU opens the normal settings list.

## Change the clock on the handset or base

1. Press **MENU**. Use the arrows to find **Set date/time**, then press **SELECT**.
2. Enter the month, day and two-digit year in that order. For November 7, 2026, the three entries are **11**, **07**, **26**. You can also choose a value with the arrows and advance with SELECT.
3. Enter the hour on the 12-hour clock, followed by the minutes. For example, use **04** and **05** for five minutes past four.
4. Choose **AM** or **PM** with the arrows. Press **SELECT** to save. Listen for the confirmation tone.

Two beeps during number entry mean the phone rejected a value. Look at the field still on screen. For example, entering 17 in the hour field will fail: enter 05 and then select PM. ## Why the answering system announces the wrong day

The DS6751 uses the date and year to calculate the weekday for a message. If the hour looks right but the answering system says Friday instead of Saturday, check the **year** as well as the month and day. If the announcement is twelve hours off, check **AM/PM**.

After saving, call the phone and leave a short new message. Play that message to check its announcement. An old recording was made under the previous clock setting. Changing the clock also leaves the ringing level as a separate control. If calls arrive silently, check the [VTech ringer settings](/guides/vtech-phone-ringer-volume).

## What caller ID changes on an incoming call

On this family, caller ID service can supply the day, month and time when a call arrives. You still need to enter the year correctly. If the clock looks right after manual entry and changes after the next incoming call, note the time immediately before and after the call. That distinguishes a saved setting from an incoming time update.

## When you hear “Time and date not set”

This announcement precedes playback when the clock had no setting as the message was recorded. Complete the date and time, including the last SELECT, then use a fresh message to verify it. If **Set date/time** is absent altogether, read the model number on the base. **DECT 6.0** identifies a cordless technology used by many VTech models.
