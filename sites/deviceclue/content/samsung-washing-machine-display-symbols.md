# What do the symbols on a Samsung washing machine display mean?

A **plain padlock or key** means the washer door is locked. A **face or smiling padlock** means Child Lock is on and the controls are restricted. A **drum with sparkles** is a reminder to run the drum-cleaning programme. These are common Samsung designs. If the picture on your panel looks different, match it to the diagram for your model. [Samsung UK’s illustrated symbol guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/what-do-the-symbols-on-my-washing-machine-mean/)

## Match the picture on the display

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain padlock or key | Door Lock | Wait for the light to go out before opening the door. |
| Face or smiling padlock | Child Lock | If the buttons ignore you, check the Child Lock setting. |
| Clock face | A delayed cycle is set | Read the label beside the control. On a **Delay End** panel, the number tells you when the wash should finish. |
| Hourglass beside a number | Time remaining | Read the number as the estimated time left in the current cycle. |
| Crossed-out bell or speaker | End-of-cycle sound is off | The wash can still finish normally. Change the sound setting if you want the tune back. |
| Tub of water with a plus sign | Extra Rinse is selected | Leave it on if you want extra rinsing, or change the option for your wash. |
| Drum with sparkles | Drum-clean reminder | Unload the washer and select the cleaning programme named on its panel. |

Compare the shape and the label beside it. For example, Samsung uses a tub with a plus for **Extra Rinse** and a tub without the plus on a **Rinse** button that sets the rinse count. [Samsung UK’s illustrated symbol guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/what-do-the-symbols-on-my-washing-machine-mean/)

## The lock is lit and the door or buttons will not respond

**If the door stays shut**, check whether the plain key or padlock is still lit. Even after you pause a front-loader, the door can stay locked while the water is too high or too hot. Let the washer drain or cool as appropriate, and wait for the indicator to go out before trying the handle. [Samsung UK’s door-opening guidance](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/how-can-i-open-the-door-of-my-washing-machine-during-operation/)

**If you cannot change a setting**, look for the face symbol. On the navigation-dial panel pictured in Samsung’s Child Lock guide, close the door, open **Additional Functions**, turn the dial to **Child Lock**, then use **Select** to choose **Off**. On button-only panels, match the button pair to the diagram for your washer. Child Lock can stay active after you turn the power off. [Samsung UK’s Child Lock instructions by panel type](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/how-do-i-switch-the-child-lock-on-my-samsung-washing-machine-on-and-off/)

## The clock is counting down but the wash has not started

On a washer labelled **Delay End**, the time includes the wash itself. If you set a three-hour delay at noon, the washer aims to finish at 3 pm. It waits before starting if the selected programme takes less than three hours. An hourglass beside the time during a running wash shows how long that cycle has left. [Samsung UK’s Delay End guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/how-do-i-set-up-the-delay-end-function-on-my-washing-machine/)

## The drum symbol flashes after a wash

On panels with **Eco Drum Clean** or **Eco Drum Clean+**, take the laundry out and run the named programme with the drum empty. Leave out ordinary laundry detergent. Samsung gives a 40-wash reminder for the Eco Drum Clean+ example in its guide. Other models can use a different interval. [Samsung UK’s drum-cleaning instructions](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/check-the-cleaning-functions-and-sections-on-the-washing-machine/)

## What if the display shows letters and numbers?

Read the characters in order. A code belongs to a particular washer family. For example, [Hitachi SF-XWV washers use C2 for drainage](/guides/hitachi-washing-machine-c1-c2-errors). Samsung UK lists **4C** and **4E** for a water-supply problem, and **5C** and **5E** for a drainage problem. The final character matters too. For example, **4C2** points to the water temperature. If the washer stops with a code, take a photo and use the entry for the complete code and your model. [Samsung UK’s washing machine code table](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/what-do-the-codes-on-my-washing-machine-mean/)
