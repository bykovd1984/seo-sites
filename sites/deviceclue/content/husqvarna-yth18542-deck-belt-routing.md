# Husqvarna YTH18542 belt routing: deck belt or motion drive belt?

On the **YTH18542, model 917.279060, product 960 13 00-02**, the two belt diagrams describe different systems. Use **Figure 20** for the mower deck and **Figure 22** for the tractor’s motion drive. Match the product number on the tractor before buying a belt. A search for “YTH18542 drive belt” can return either one as well as parts for later revisions.

## Identify which belt you are looking at

| What you need to identify | Diagram for this tractor | Where the belt goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Belt that drives the cutting blades | Figure 20, printed page 20 | Around the two blade-spindle pulleys (called mandrels) and the deck idlers |
| Belt that moves the tractor | Figure 22, printed page 21 | Between the engine and transmission, through the tractor’s idlers and guides |

If the tractor moves but the blades will not engage, the deck diagram is the relevant starting point for identifying the belt. If the problem is tractor travel, look at the motion-drive diagram. Either symptom can have other causes.

## Read the deck belt route before removing the belt

Use the [Husqvarna deck illustration, Figure 20](https://content.tdr.dss.husqvarnagroup.net/pub000027523/doc000054528#page=20) with the same orientation as the deck. It labels both mandrel pulleys and the idler pulleys. If the old belt is still in place, photograph its path and each guide. If it has already come off, use the illustration to trace the loop rather than guessing from the pulley positions.

For service, park on level ground, disengage the attachment, put the motion control in neutral, set the brake, stop the engine and remove the key. Wait for moving parts to stop and disconnect the spark-plug wire so it cannot contact the plug. The manufacturer’s belt procedure starts with the deck removed using its removal instructions.

## Seat the replacement inside the guides

With the deck safely removed, the documented procedure takes the old belt off both mandrels and the idlers. Install the replacement along the Figure 20 path, then check each pulley groove and each belt guide before reinstalling the deck. A belt on the outside of a guide can look correctly routed when viewed from above. Refit the deck and its attachments as specified before operating the tractor.

## Check the part number or the tractor-drive diagram

For this product number, the original parts list calls the 42-inch mower belt **532 17 48-83**, item **68**. That identifies the original deck part; confirm any superseding number with the supplier. [Husqvarna’s illustrated parts list](https://www-static-nw.husqvarna.com/hbd/tdrdownload/v2/pub000027219/doc000053365/IPL/3psNkmFSEqFZUJdKAMhgKBKbEug?httproute=True)

If the belt in question reaches the transmission cooling fan area, leave the deck part number aside. Use Figure 22 for the motion drive; Husqvarna also identifies a routing decal underneath the left footrest on this version. The guides, route and replacement procedure differ.
