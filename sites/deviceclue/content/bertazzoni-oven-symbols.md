## What do Bertazzoni oven symbols mean?

The symbols select cooking functions such as bake, convection, broil and defrost, but their exact appearance and available settings depend on the oven. The most reliable answer comes from matching the **model and control panel** to Bertazzoni's function chart. The examples below describe the manufacturer's XT and XE oven reference; they are not a symbol key for every Bertazzoni range.

Look for the model label and compare the entire icon, not just a fan or a line. A similar-looking symbol can represent a different combination of heat and airflow on another product.

## XT and XE functions: bake, fan and broil

| Function in the XT/XE reference | What the manufacturer describes |
|---|---|
| Bake | Upper and lower heating elements. |
| Convection | Circular heating elements and the convection fan. |
| Turbo | Upper and circular heating elements with the convection fan. |
| Broil | The broil element, with selectable power levels. |
| Convection broil | Broil and upper heating elements with the convection fan. |
| Defrost / dehydrate | The fan operates; in the documented defrost mode no heating element is on, while dehydrate also uses the lower element. |

The manufacturer's [XT/XE reference chart](https://us.bertazzoni.com/media/userfiles/files/Quick%20reference%20guide_XT%20and%20XE%20ovens.pdf) shows the corresponding illustrations. Compare it only if the oven belongs to that family; a different control panel needs its own chart. In particular, do not infer that every symbol containing a fan is a no-heat defrost setting.

## Is the fan still running after cooking a fault?

Bertazzoni's US FAQ says fans may continue operating after an oven is switched off as part of cooling and air circulation. That observation alone does not identify a fault. If you notice smoke, a burning smell, unusual noise or behaviour that concerns you, switch the appliance off as appropriate and contact an authorised service provider.

## What does the cleaning setting do?

The XT/XE chart lists a Clean function with two modes. Cleaning systems and preparation vary: do not assume that a symbol on a different model starts the same cycle or that racks can remain inside. Check your exact product's cleaning directions before starting a high-temperature cycle.

## How can I identify an unfamiliar icon?

Find the model number and look up that exact product in Bertazzoni's specification library or contact its support. Photograph the control panel and include the model when asking for help. If the label or icon is worn, do not select a cleaning or high-heat setting based only on a visual guess.
