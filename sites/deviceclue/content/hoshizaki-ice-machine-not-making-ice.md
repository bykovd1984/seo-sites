## Why is my Hoshizaki ice machine not making ice?

Start by distinguishing **no power, no production, slow production and a machine that makes ice but will not dispense it**. These are different symptoms. Check the full model number and note any repeating alarm before turning the machine off or calling service.

Some Hoshizaki machines pause when the storage bin is full, and some installations use a drain pump that can stop an ice maker under certain conditions. Other causes need a technician to test; a symptom alone cannot identify a failed part.

## A safe first check

| What you observe | What to do |
|---|---|
| Bin is full | Check whether production resumes after normal ice use. Do not defeat the bin control. |
| Machine has no visible power | Check the user-accessible power switch and the building supply without opening electrical covers. If a breaker repeatedly trips, stop and arrange service. |
| Ice production is slow | Note the room conditions, visible airflow obstructions and the maintenance history; report these with the model. |
| A repeating beep is present | Count the pattern before switching anything off. Meanings vary by control and model. |
| Ice exists in the bin but does not dispense | Treat dispensing as a separate problem; check the applicable user instructions and arrange service if needed. |
| Water is leaking, or electrical parts are wet | Avoid the wet area and isolate power and water only if it is safe to do so; contact service. |

Check whether the external water supply is available and whether a user-accessible shutoff has been closed, if it is safe to inspect. Do not disconnect water lines, open panels or probe electrical components. Never assume a blocked filter or low refrigerant is the cause without a proper diagnosis.

## Does an alarm explain why there is no ice?

An alarm narrows the investigation but does not automatically identify a replacement part. On documented KM-style controls, repeating patterns can describe a safety or cycle-duration condition. Count the beeps and see the [Hoshizaki beeping guide](/guides/hoshizaki-ice-machine-beeping); match its examples to your own machine before relying on them. Do not repeatedly reset a protective alarm to keep production going.

## What about a full drain pump?

Hoshizaki’s HS-5061 drain-pump bulletin describes certain AM-50, C-80BAJ, C-101 and C-100 installations in which an ice maker stops when the pump is full and restarts after the level drops. This is a **specific installation example**, not an explanation for every Hoshizaki machine. Inspection of pump connections and internal components belongs to a qualified service person.

## Should I clean or reset the machine?

Routine cleaning depends on the model, water conditions and installation. Use the manufacturer’s prescribed procedure and schedule; do not mix chemicals or substitute an unverified cleaning method. Commercial equipment may require trained personnel and food-safety procedures.

Before any reset, record the model, alarm pattern and conditions. A cleared alarm does not establish that its cause is fixed. If the machine has no ice after basic user-accessible checks, contact a qualified technician with those details, the maintenance history and whether water or ice is present in the bin.

For the difference between a control-board reset and a user power switch, see the [Hoshizaki alarm reset guide](/guides/hoshizaki-ice-machine-reset-button).
