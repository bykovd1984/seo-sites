# How to set the clock on a Miele M 8260-2 microwave

On the Miele M 8260-2, use the **clock-face button** and the dial marked for **time and weight**. The separate minute-minder button sets a countdown. These controls are shown in the Australian and New Zealand manual. [Miele control-panel diagram, page 5](https://www.miele.com.au/forms2/au/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=07679380#page=5)

## Set the time when 12:00 flashes

After the microwave is connected to power, **12:00** and the clock symbol flash. Turn the time selector to the hour, press the clock button, turn the selector to the minutes, then press the clock button again. The two dots between the hour and minutes blink when the time has been saved. [Miele initial clock setup, page 20](https://www.miele.com.au/forms2/au/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=07679380#page=20)

## When the hour is wrong

1. Give the clock button a short press. The clock symbol flashes and the current hour lights up.
2. While the hour is lit, use the time selector to change it. Press the clock button to move to the minutes.
3. Turn the selector if the minutes need changing. Press the clock button to save. Waiting a few seconds also accepts the displayed value.

If the minutes already show the correct value, leave the dial where it is and confirm. [Miele instructions for changing the time, page 20](https://www.miele.com.au/forms2/au/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=07679380#page=20)

## When the clock has disappeared

If the microwave responds but the idle display is dark, press and hold the clock button to bring back a time display that was manually hidden. Holding that button also hides the time, which is why a brief press matters when you only want to correct the hour. [Miele time-of-day settings, page 20](https://www.miele.com.au/forms2/au/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=07679380#page=20)

## Keep the clock visible overnight

If the display disappears **only between 11 p.m. and 4 a.m.**, check the night setting. Hold **Stop/C** and press the clock button at the same time. **OFF** means the overnight switch-off has been disabled and the clock can stay visible. **ON** means the overnight switch-off is enabled. The microwave can still be used during those hours. [Miele night-time display instructions, page 20](https://www.miele.com.au/forms2/au/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=07679380#page=20)
