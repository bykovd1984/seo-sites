# What Black Gamma changes on a Sony α6600 or α7 IV

**Black Gamma** changes how a Sony Picture Profile renders dark tones. On the **α6600** and **α7 IV**, **Range** chooses which tones the adjustment reaches. **Level** sets how much to brighten or darken them. For example, if the folds in a black jacket blend together, a small positive Level can help you see the folds. Start with **Narrow** to judge the effect near black. [Sony’s illustrated guide to Black Gamma](https://helpguide.sony.net/di/pp/v1/en/contents/TP0000909110.html) · [Sony α6600 Picture Profile controls](https://helpguide.sony.net/ilc/1940/v1/en/contents/TP0002679609.html)

## Where to find Black Gamma on your camera

On the **α6600 (ILCE-6600)**, open **MENU → Camera Settings1 → Picture Profile**. On the **α7 IV (ILCE-7M4)**, open **MENU → Exposure/Color → Color/Tone → Picture Profile**. Highlight the profile you intend to edit and press the right side of the control wheel. Choose **Black Gamma**. If the profile already gives you a look you depend on, use the **Copy** command to copy it into a spare profile slot first. Check the destination before confirming. [Sony α6600 profile editing](https://helpguide.sony.net/ilc/1940/v1/en/contents/TP0002679609.html) · [Sony α7 IV profile editing](https://helpguide.sony.net/ilc/2110/v1/en/contents/TP1000649066.html)

## What Narrow, Middle and Wide do

**Narrow** concentrates the adjustment near black. **Wide** extends it into gray, and **Middle** falls between them. Imagine the jacket against a gray wall. If you want to change the folds while keeping the wall close to its original tone, try Narrow. If the wall should change along with the jacket, compare Wide at the same Level. The brightness of the wall in the actual shot determines how much it changes. [Sony’s comparison of Black Gamma ranges](https://helpguide.sony.net/di/pp/v1/en/contents/TP0000909110.html)

## Compare Level with the same shot

Keep the lighting, exposure and framing fixed. Record a short reference at **Level 0**, then compare a small positive adjustment and a small negative one. Look at the seam in the jacket and an adjacent patch of dark fabric. A positive setting lifts the affected tones. A negative setting darkens them and can make those two patches harder to distinguish. The α7 IV scale runs from **−7 to +7**. **Black Level** is a separate control for the black level of the image. Leave it in place during this comparison. [Sony’s Black Level and Black Gamma examples](https://helpguide.sony.net/di/pp/v1/en/contents/TP0000909110.html)

## Why Black Gamma can be fixed at zero

Check **Gamma** in the active profile. On both cameras, **HLG, HLG1, HLG2 and HLG3** lock Black Gamma at zero. The factory **PP10** profile uses HLG2. If you need that HDR gamma for your delivery, leave the profile in place. For a separate comparison, an unused profile with **Movie** gamma lets you try the adjustment. If you are comparing RAW stills, Sony also documents that Black Gamma is omitted when RAW files are developed using the shooting settings. Use a recorded movie to judge the video profile.
