## How do you reset a Honeywell thermostat?

First decide **what you need to reset**. A schedule reset replaces the programmed timetable; a Wi-Fi reset addresses network settings; a factory reset can remove a much wider set of settings. The controls and consequences depend on the exact Honeywell Home model. A blank screen, a scheduling question and a Wi-Fi problem do not automatically require a factory reset.

For the documented **T6 Pro Smart thermostat**, the regular RESET menu has a SCHEDULE option. Factory reset is in a separate advanced menu described in the installer documentation. Do not apply this menu sequence to every thermostat labelled Pro Series.

## Reset the schedule on a T6 Pro Smart

The following is the manufacturer's example for the T6 Pro Smart, not all T6 or Pro models. Note your preferred times and temperatures before continuing: the action restores the default Monday–Friday and Saturday–Sunday schedule.

1. Touch MENU on the thermostat screen.
2. Use the arrows to find RESET, then touch SELECT.
3. Use the arrows to find SCHEDULE, then touch SELECT.
4. Choose YES to confirm. Review the restored schedule and adjust it to suit your home.

If your screen or menu differs, stop and identify the model instead of guessing. If you merely want manual control, the same T6 Pro Smart documentation describes turning scheduling off; that is a different action from resetting its schedule.

## Is a factory reset necessary?

Usually investigate the specific problem first. The manufacturer's T6 Pro Smart professional guide places factory reset in the advanced menu, alongside installer settings for the heating and cooling equipment. Restoring defaults may require the thermostat to be configured for the installed system again. Record relevant settings and contact the installer or a qualified HVAC technician before proceeding if you do not know how the system is configured.

Do not change wiring or unrelated installer options while following a general reset guide. If heat or cooling is behaving abnormally, the screen alone cannot identify the failed component.

## Reset, restart and battery replacement are different

Restarting the device may resolve a temporary interruption but is not necessarily a factory reset. If a battery warning appears, use the correct procedure and cells for the actual product: our [Honeywell battery replacement guide](/guides/honeywell-thermostat-battery-replacement) separates two documented T6 models with different batteries. Do not remove a thermostat or turn off equipment without following the relevant product instructions.

## What if the thermostat says recovery?

Recovery can be a normal scheduling feature rather than an error. Check the next scheduled temperature and time before resetting anything. Our [Honeywell recovery mode guide](/guides/honeywell-thermostat-recovery-mode) explains that distinction and a documented model-specific way to change the feature.
