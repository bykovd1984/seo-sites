## Can I remove oven door glass for cleaning?

Some conventional ovens have removable inner panes, but the fasteners, pane order and hinge arrangements depend on the model. Find the model and product number and follow the manufacturer's instructions for that exact appliance. Let the oven and door cool completely first. Never operate it with a pane or the door removed.

Do not treat a microwave or a combination microwave oven like a conventional oven. Zanussi says its microwave and combi oven doors should be removed only by an authorised technician because of safety. If the glass is cracked, the hinge is damaged, or you cannot identify the correct instructions, stop and arrange service.

## Before touching the door

1. Check the appliance type, model and product number. Locate the manufacturer's instructions for removing the panes, not just the outer door.
2. Let the appliance cool fully. Prepare a flat, padded surface for any part the instructions say can be removed.
3. Check whether the door can remain fitted while the panes are removed. Some designs require a different sequence; do not force trim, clips or screws.
4. Record the original order and orientation of each pane and spacer before moving it, if the model instructions allow removal. Keep fasteners together.

If the door must come off, be aware that spring-loaded hinges can snap shut. Do not put fingers near a hinge or attempt to force it open. A second person or a technician may be appropriate for a heavy or awkward door.

## Example: a Zanussi door with a removable top strip

Zanussi describes an example of a conventional single oven with a plastic strip across the top of the door. On the pictured arrangement, textured areas indicate where to press and pull the strip to release the panes. Clean and refit the panes in their original positions, then replace the retaining strip as directed.

This is a **panel-specific example**, not a universal method for every Zanussi oven or other brand. Some doors use other retainers or cannot be dismantled by the owner. Check the manufacturer's illustrations against the actual door before acting.

## What should I use to clean the glass?

For the Zanussi example, the manufacturer suggests starting with a wet cloth and using non-abrasive products. Follow the glass and cleaner instructions for your appliance. Avoid hard scrapers, abrasive pads, sudden temperature changes and impacts, particularly on the edges of the glass. Dry the panes before reassembly as specified by the manufacturer.

Condensation between panes can sometimes clear as the oven dries. If moisture is inside a microwave or combination oven door, do not dismantle it: consult the manufacturer or an authorised technician.

## Before using the oven again

Check that every pane, seal and spacer is in its original position and that the door and retainers are fully secured according to the model instructions. If anything is loose, a pane is chipped, or a hinge has snapped back, leave the oven off and obtain service.
