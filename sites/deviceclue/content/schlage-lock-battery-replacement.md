## How do you change the battery in a Schlage lock?

Find the lock model first. The **Schlage BE365 keypad deadbolt** uses one 9-volt battery under its interior cover; other Schlage locks use different battery arrangements and covers. Schlage recommends a high-quality alkaline battery for the BE365.

Keep the door open while you work and test the lock before closing it. Have a working physical key available if your model has a key cylinder. Do not assume these steps apply to an Encode, Sense, lever or touchscreen model.

## Replace the battery in a BE365 keypad deadbolt

You need a Phillips screwdriver and one new alkaline 9-volt battery. Schlage's BE365 instructions give the following sequence:

1. From the inside of the door, remove the two screws holding the interior cover and lift the cover off.
2. Disconnect the old battery from its connector. Connect the new 9-volt battery, aligning the terminals with the connector.
3. Put the battery securely in its compartment. Tuck the wires so the cover will not pinch them.
4. Replace the inside cover and its screws. With the door still open, check that a valid code operates the lock and that the mechanical thumbturn moves freely.

Do not pull hard on the wires or force the connector. A damaged connector, corrosion, heat or leakage needs attention before the lock is returned to service. If operation is unreliable, use the key if applicable and contact Schlage or a locksmith.

## What battery does another Schlage lock need?

Check the product label and Schlage's instructions for that specific model. A 9-volt battery and the two-screw cover described here are **BE365-specific**; a different keypad or smart lock may use AA batteries, a different cover or a separate battery holder. Match polarity and replace the full set as the manufacturer directs. Do not mix old and new cells unless the manufacturer explicitly permits it.

## Will replacing the battery erase my codes?

A battery change is a maintenance operation; it is not the same step as a factory reset. Do not press a reset control simply because you are changing a battery. Before relying on the lock, test your existing code with the door open. If codes or app access no longer work, consult the support instructions for that model rather than repeatedly trying random programming sequences.

## Is every beep a low-battery warning?

No. A sound or light can have different meanings by model and by the action being performed. Compare the exact warning with your lock's own guidance. If the keypad is unresponsive, a new battery is one practical check, but it does not establish the cause of every fault.
