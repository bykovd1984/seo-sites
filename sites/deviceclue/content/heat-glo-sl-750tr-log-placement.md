# Heat & Glo SL-750TR log placement by model and log set

Read the entire model number from the fireplace rating plate before comparing photos. **SL-750TR-D and SL-950TR-D use the six-piece LOGS-SL750-D set.** The older **SL-750TR without a suffix uses a five-piece LOGS-750 set**. Both can turn up in a search for “SL 750TR log placement.” The sixth log in a D-series photo belongs there only if the plate and log set match.

| SL-750TR-D or SL-950TR-D | LOGS-SL750-D, six logs | [Three-page Heat & Glo photo sequence](https://downloads.hearthnhome.com/logs/2045_936.PDF) |
| SL-550TR-D | LOGS-SL550-D, five logs | [D-series illustrated guide, printed pages 43–45](https://downloads.hearthnhome.com/installManuals/2044_985.PDF#page=43) |
| SL-750TR or SL-750TR-DSI | LOGS-750, five logs | [Original model parts drawing, first page](https://downloads.hearthnhome.com/serviceParts/SL-750TR.pdf#page=1) |

The older parts drawing identifies the five pieces by number and shape. It is an exploded view. For service on that version, give the dealer the full model and serial number so they can match its installation instructions. Suffixes such as **-C** and **-E** also identify different versions.

## Where do the six D-series logs go?

Read the photos looking into the firebox from the front. The large rear log is **1**. Its locating notches sit on the grate. The manufacturer shows a different fore-and-aft position depending on the fuel marked on the appliance. For natural gas the log sits against the front tabs. For propane the top of the log reaches the rear firebox wall. Moving this piece changes where the upper logs will land.

At the front, **2** sits on the two right-hand grate bars, back against its tab. **3** sits on the two left-hand bars, in line with 2. The lower pair run parallel to the rear log. The first two pages of the photo sheet show the underside notches and the grate before the upper pieces cover them.

## How do I identify the three upper pieces?

| Number in the six-log photos | Original part | Contact points in the finished layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | SRV2045-703 | Connects the locator on 1 to the flat seat on 2 |
| 5 | SRV2045-702 | Fits the tab on 1 and the raised seats on 2 |
| 6 | SRV2045-701 | Left end on 3, middle at the tab on 1, right end in the notch on 5 |

If the last piece does not line up, compare **1, 2 and 5** with the earlier photos. A generic image of a finished fire can hide all three contact points. The smaller SL-550TR-D has its own arrangement and its front logs sit forward against the grate. Its five pieces cannot be identified using the six-log numbering. ## What if a log is cracked, displaced or does not match?

Compare the visible arrangement through the glass with the appliance off and fully cool. Heat & Glo says a correctly factory-installed set normally needs no repositioning. If a piece is broken, a log no longer seats at the pictured points, or soot has appeared after a log moved, leave the fireplace off and have a qualified fireplace technician check it. The technician should verify the set, fuel marking, placement and glass seal before use. Operate only with the fixed glass secured and the protective barrier fitted.
