## Which Defy fridge setting is coldest?

On the **Defy C380, C425 and C455 examples** in the cited manufacturer guide, the thermostat has settings **1 to 5**, and 5 is the coldest. The guide recommends starting at a middle setting and adjusting after checking the temperature. That scale is a cooling control: its numbers are not temperatures in degrees.

Other Defy models have different controls. A display showing a set temperature in °C works differently: a lower number of degrees represents a colder target. Do not apply the 1–5 answer to a different dial, a 1–7 scale or a digital display without identifying the model.

## How to set a Defy fridge with a 1–5 dial

1. Find the full model identification on the appliance and compare the thermostat with the instructions for that model and revision.
2. If it matches the documented C380/C425/C455 1–5 thermostat, start around the middle of the scale as the manufacturer recommends.
3. Use a fridge thermometer to check the actual compartment temperature after the appliance has had time to stabilise. Adjust gradually if needed and check again.
4. If food freezes in the fresh-food compartment, a less cold setting may be appropriate; if it remains too warm, check loading, door closure and the measured temperature as well as the dial.

The dial is not a thermometer. A particular number does not guarantee a particular food temperature, and a newly adjusted refrigerator needs time to settle. For food safety, the FDA recommends keeping a refrigerator at or below 40°F / 4°C and a freezer at 0°F / −18°C; measure rather than infer the result from the dial.

## What about a Defy 1–7 setting?

We have not verified a universal Defy 1–7 mapping. If the model’s instructions explicitly describe a numbered thermostat as cooling intensity, use that model’s direction. If the panel instead shows degrees Celsius, the reading is a temperature target. Look up the exact model before assuming that 7 means colder or that the numbers represent degrees.

The DFD448 documentation, for example, describes a digital temperature setting; it is a different style of control and does not prove how a two-door 1–7 dial works. Our [general fridge dial guide](/guides/fridge-coldest-setting) explains the distinction between temperature and cooling-strength scales.

## Fridge warm but freezer cold?

Record the temperatures in both compartments and check that doors close and vents are not blocked. A warm fridge can have causes beyond the selected setting. Do not remove electrical covers or attempt refrigerant repairs; contact qualified service if the temperature remains unsafe after basic checks.
