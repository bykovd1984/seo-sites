# How to thread a Singer 4423 and wind the bobbin

To thread a Singer Heavy Duty 4423, raise the needle and presser foot, follow the **upper thread path through the guides, tension area and take-up lever**, then thread the needle. The bobbin is wound and inserted separately. Match each guide to the illustrations for the 4423: a missed take-up lever or tension path can cause loose stitches even when thread reaches the needle.

Switch the machine off before threading the needle. Keep fingers away from the needle and moving parts, and do not press the foot control while placing thread. These instructions describe the documented 4423; other Singer machines can have a different path or bobbin system.

## Thread the upper path

1. Raise the presser foot. Turn the handwheel **towards you**, not away, until the needle reaches its highest point and just begins to descend. Check that the take-up lever is raised.
2. Place the thread spool on the spool pin and secure it with the appropriate spool holder.
3. Lead the thread through the upper guide and pre-tension spring shown in the 4423 diagram.
4. Bring it down the right-hand channel through the tension area, then up the left-hand channel and through the take-up lever from right to left.
5. Bring the thread down through the lower guides and the needle guide. With the machine switched off, thread the needle from front to back.

The small guides are easy to miss. Compare the actual path with the manufacturer's diagram rather than forcing thread into a space that only looks like a guide. The separate automatic needle threader has its own procedure; do not force it if the needle or hook is not aligned.

## Wind the bobbin

Use the bobbin type specified for your machine. Singer warns that similarly shaped metal and transparent bobbins are not automatically interchangeable.

1. Follow the 4423 winding diagram from the spool through the bobbin-winding tension guide.
2. Feed thread through the bobbin hole as illustrated, put the bobbin on the winding spindle and move the spindle to its winding position.
3. Hold the free end while starting as directed in the model instructions. Keep hands clear of moving parts.
4. When the bobbin is wound, stop, return the spindle to its sewing position and cut the thread.

An uneven or loose bobbin can cause problems. Make sure the thread passes through the winding tension guide rather than bypassing it; consult the model diagram for the exact route.

## Insert and bring up the bobbin thread

With the machine switched off, the presser foot raised and the needle fully raised, remove the bobbin cover. Insert the bobbin with its thread running counterclockwise as shown in the 4423 diagram. Lead the thread through slit A and clockwise into notch B while holding the bobbin down. Leave about six inches of thread towards the back and refit the cover without trapping it.

Hold the upper thread lightly with your left hand. Turn the handwheel towards you through one full needle movement, lowering and raising the needle. Gently pull the upper thread to bring up the bobbin loop, then place both thread tails under the presser foot toward the back. If the loop will not come up, check that the bobbin thread is not trapped and that the upper path is complete.

## Thread bunching or breaking?

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| Loops under fabric | Rethread the upper path with the presser foot raised; check the take-up lever. |
| No bobbin thread comes up | Check bobbin orientation, the slot and whether thread is trapped. |
| Bobbin winds loosely | Check the winding tension guide and compatible bobbin. |
| Needle thread breaks | Recheck the thread path and the needle condition and installation. |

The 4423 instructions say bobbin tension is factory set. Do not begin by turning the bobbin-case screw. Test on scrap fabric and seek service if the machine jams or the needle mechanism is damaged.
