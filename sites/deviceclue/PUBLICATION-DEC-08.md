## Editorial checks

Manufacturer documentation was checked on 6 November 2026. Every article has at least two source references, a direct answer and explicit applicability limits. No equipment testing or independent technical review is claimed.

- VTech distinguishes ringer, listening volume, base volume and Quiet mode; no universal DECT 6.0 button sequence.
- AT&T uses a TL86109 restart example and keeps DLP73210 registration separate.
- Avaya restricts *09 to IP Office defaults and warns that *00 changes other forwarding settings.
- Mitel separates transfer from forwarding, and Office 250 from Communications Director/SIP. Contextual links join the two tasks.
- HP distinguishes Officejet E709/E710 from ENVY/DeskJet 6500 and cartridge replacement from printhead removal. HP's HTML replacement page returned 403; official E709/E710 PDFs supplied the procedure instead.
- Kenmore distinguishes low HE water level from no fill; no live electrical testing, sensor adjustments or lock bypasses.
- Hitachi uses the exact SF-240XWV/200XWV/180XWV code table and avoids transferring drain-height rules to unrelated washers.
- POWERbot separates charge/docking checks from wheel/bumper codes, with contextual links. Samsung's maintenance-page introductory switch wording conflicts with its own wheel steps and code support; the article follows the explicit switch-off instructions for debris removal.
