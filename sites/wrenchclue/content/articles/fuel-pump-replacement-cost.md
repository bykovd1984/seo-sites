# Fuel Pump Replacement Cost, Labor, and Repair Time

Fuel pump replacement has a broad US estimated cost of **$1,344–$1,513**, according to RepairPal's September 3, 2026 update, checked November 8, 2026. The parts account for most of that estimate. To find out what your repair will cost and when you can pick up the vehicle, confirm which pump failed, what the replacement assembly includes, and how the technician reaches it. An in-tank supply pump and an engine-mounted high-pressure pump are different jobs. [RepairPal fuel pump replacement estimate](https://repairpal.com/estimator/fuel-pump-replacement-cost)

## What the national estimate includes

| Estimate item | Published US range | Detail to match on your quote |
|---|---:|---|
| Parts | $1,104–$1,160 | Pump location, assembly contents, manufacturer, and part number |
| Labor | $241–$353 | Installation work covered for your engine and vehicle configuration |
| Replacement total | $1,344–$1,513 | Whether diagnosis, related work, local charges, and tax appear separately |

These are RepairPal's published rounded figures. The total excludes taxes, fees, location adjustments, and related repairs. Use the published total as a starting point for comparison, without trying to reconstruct an exact bill by adding the ends of the rounded ranges. A supplier's price for a pump you can buy over the counter covers only the item in the box. [RepairPal estimate details](https://repairpal.com/estimator/fuel-pump-replacement-cost)

When a quote differs substantially from the range, look first at its largest line. If most of the difference is in parts, compare part numbers and assembly contents. If it is in labor, ask how much covers diagnosis, access, installation, and any additional work. Those questions can explain a difference even between shops serving the same ZIP code.

## Identify the pump and what comes with it

On a vehicle with separate supply and high-pressure pumps, fuel passes through two stages. The supply pump delivers fuel from the tank. The high-pressure pump raises pressure for injection on an engine designed that way. Ask the service writer to put the failed stage in writing. A price returned for a general fuel pump search gives you little basis for deciding whether a high-pressure pump quote is reasonable.

For an in-tank repair, distinguish the electric pump itself from a complete pump module. Depending on the application, a module can include the housing, strainer, and fuel-level sender. Ask whether the quote is for a complete module or a pump fitted to an existing assembly, which pieces are reused, and whether a seal or required wiring kit is included. Two listings that both say *fuel pump* can cover different amounts of hardware and installation work. [Delphi fuel pump service guidance](https://www.delphiautoparts.com/products-technologies/gasoline-fuel-systems/fuel-management-system/fuel-modules-and-pumps/how-to-perform-a-voltage-drop-test)

Give the shop the model year, engine, and VIN when asking for a price. An estimate for a Chevy Silverado is a poor stand-in for one on a 2004 Avalanche, even if both listings describe a GM truck fuel pump. The same issue arises when searching by a name such as Ford Ranger that spans several generations. The vehicle details let the parts department identify the actual assembly before quoting it.

## What changes the labor and pickup time

For a tank-mounted pump, access is a substantial part of the job. Some vehicle designs provide a service opening. Others require the shop to lower the fuel tank to reach the assembly. An engine-mounted pump has a different access route and installation procedure. Ask which operation the shop priced, especially if one estimate mentions tank removal and another simply lists pump installation.

Billed labor and time without the car answer different questions. Labor covers the work listed on the estimate. Pickup time also depends on when diagnosis finishes, whether the correct assembly is on hand, and when the shop can complete its checks after installation. You cannot convert RepairPal's labor charge into a dependable number of hours without the applicable labor rate and job details. [RepairPal replacement cost breakdown](https://repairpal.com/estimator/fuel-pump-replacement-cost)

If you need transportation for work, ask for the expected pickup time *after* the shop has identified the pump and confirmed parts availability. Ask what finding would change that timing. For example, contamination discovered in the tank can require attention before the new pump goes into service. Have the shop explain the finding and price the additional work separately. [Delphi fuel pump guidance](https://www.delphiautoparts.com/products-technologies/gasoline-fuel-systems/fuel-management-system/fuel-modules-and-pumps/how-to-perform-a-voltage-drop-test)

## What justifies buying the pump

Low fuel delivery can result from a pump motor problem, a restriction, or an electrical supply problem. A poor connection or ground can leave a working pump with insufficient voltage. Before authorizing the part, ask what the measured pressure or flow showed under the vehicle's specified test conditions and whether the technician confirmed the pump received the electrical supply it needed. On a two-pump system, ask which stage the measurements implicate. [Delphi diagnostic guidance](https://www.delphiautoparts.com/products-technologies/gasoline-fuel-systems/fuel-management-system/fuel-modules-and-pumps/how-to-perform-a-voltage-drop-test)

Describe a starting complaint precisely. If the starter turns the engine but the engine never catches, fuel delivery is one possibility. If the engine does not turn over at all, tell the shop that before requesting a pump estimate. The diagnostic work begins elsewhere for a [car that won't crank](car-wont-crank.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a fuel filter a less expensive fix for the same problem?** A clogged filter can mimic poor pump output. Ask whether testing identified a restriction and whether the filter is separately serviceable on your vehicle. A filter change addresses a different fault from a failed pump motor. [RepairPal fuel pump guide](https://repairpal.com/estimator/fuel-pump-replacement-cost)

**What should I ask if the cheaper quote is for a bare pump?** Ask which existing parts the installer plans to reuse, whether the pump is specified for that assembly, and what the installed total includes. Compare that figure with the written total for a complete module.

**Does a diagnostic charge count toward the repair?** Ask each shop how it handles the charge. Request the total due if you authorize replacement and the amount due if you take the diagnosis elsewhere. That makes the two choices comparable before you commit.

**What if I smell gasoline after picking up the vehicle?** If there's a strong gasoline odor or a visible fuel leak, pull over safely if driving, shut off the engine, and leave it off. Arrange inspection and towing rather than driving back to the shop. [RepairPal fuel pump replacement guidance](https://repairpal.com/estimator/fuel-pump-replacement-cost)
